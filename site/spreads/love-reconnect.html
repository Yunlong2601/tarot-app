Will My Ex Come Back?

7 cards · Love

Best for

Reflecting on a past relationship from the user's side: what happened inside them and what they can change. Never predict whether anyone returns and never advise contacting anyone.

Positions

  1. What ended
  2. What I am still carrying
  3. What I need to understand
  4. What I can change
  5. What is outside my control
  6. What healing could look like
  7. A next step for me
