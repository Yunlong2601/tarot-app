Spreads

Yes or No

3 cards · Classics

Best for

A yes or no question. The app computes a lean from the cards: upright leans yes, reversed leans no, and the middle card counts double. Explain the lean as a tendency the cards show, never a certain answer, and say what could shift it.

Positions

  1. First card
  2. The heart of it (counts double)
  3. Third card

Source: A common yes or no method: upright cards lean yes, reversed cards lean no, and the middle card counts double. Reversed cards are always on for this spread.
