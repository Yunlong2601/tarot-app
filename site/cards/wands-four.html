Four of Wands

Minor Arcana · Wands · Element: Fire

celebrationstabilityhome

A celebration of reaching a stable point.

A moment of rest and shared joy after effort.

What the card shows

Four tall wands are linked by a garland of flowers and fruit, forming a gateway. Two figures raise bouquets, with people dancing and a castle behind them.

Four is the number of foundations. The four wands form a frame, a structure to celebrate in. Garlands and the castle suggest home, community and a milestone reached.

Upright

A moment of harmony and shared joy after effort.

Light: celebration, belonging, security. Shadow: complacency, clinging to the party.

Love: Togetherness, a milestone, a feeling of home.

Work: Finishing a stage; being recognised by a team.

To reflect on: What is worth celebrating right now?

Reversed

Instability at home, or a celebration that feels hollow.

  • Blocked: unsettled foundations.
  • Fading: a good time ending.
  • Inward: joy not shared.

To reflect on: What would make this place feel like home?
