Ace of Cups

Minor Arcana · Cups · Element: Water

opennessnew feelingscompassion

A feeling that wants to overflow.

An opening of the heart. Welcome kindness and emotional renewal.

What the card shows

A hand from a cloud holds a cup, from which five streams pour into a pond covered in lilies. A dove carrying a small wafer descends toward the cup.

Aces are the seed of their suit. Water is feeling, so this is the opening of the heart. Overflowing water shows abundance, the lilies show calm, and the dove adds peace and grace.

Upright

A new emotional beginning: love, compassion, creativity or connection.

Light: openness, compassion, new love. Shadow: feeling that is not acknowledged.

Love: A fresh start in love or deeper closeness.

Work: Passion for work that fits your values.

To reflect on: What would I let myself feel if I were not guarding it?

Reversed

Feelings are held in, blocked or poured out unseen.

  • Blocked: closed off emotionally.
  • Excess: overflowing with no direction.
  • Inward: feelings kept private.

To reflect on: What feeling am I keeping from myself?
