Five of Pentacles

Minor Arcana · Pentacles · Element: Earth

hardshipworryfeeling left out

Feeling left out in the cold.

A lean time. Help exists if you reach for it.

What the card shows

Two ragged figures, one on crutches, walk through snow past a lit stained-glass window of a church. The window shows five coins.

Five is a number of hardship. The warm window shows help is near but not noticed, and the figures keep walking. It is a picture of need and of overlooked shelter.

Upright

Hardship or feeling excluded, with help nearby.

Light: resilience, asking for help. Shadow: isolation, pride that blocks help.

Love: Feeling alone in a bond.

Work: Financial or job worries.

To reflect on: Who could I ask for help?

Reversed

Recovery, or refusing help.

  • Releasing: the worst is over.
  • Blocked: too proud to ask.
  • Excess: dwelling on scarcity.

To reflect on: What help is near that I have not asked for?
