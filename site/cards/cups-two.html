Two of Cups

Minor Arcana · Cups · Element: Water

connectionpartnershipmutual respect

A meeting of equals.

A balanced bond built on respect and shared feeling.

What the card shows

A man and a woman face each other and hold out cups in a pledge. Above them floats a winged lion head over a staff entwined with two snakes. A house stands on a hill behind.

Two is the number of pairs. This is a picture of mutual exchange: each person offers a cup and each receives. The winged lion and snake staff are old symbols of strong bonds and healing.

Upright

Connection built on mutual respect, in love or friendship.

Light: partnership, attraction, balance. Shadow: an unequal exchange, idealising the other.

Love: A bond with give and take on both sides.

Work: A good working partnership.

To reflect on: Is what I give and what I receive in balance?

Reversed

Imbalance, miscommunication or a bond drifting apart.

  • Blocked: unable to open up to the other.
  • Excess: merging too fast.
  • Fading: closeness cooling.

To reflect on: Where is the exchange between us uneven?
