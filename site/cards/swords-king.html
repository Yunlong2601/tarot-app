King of Swords

Minor Arcana · Swords · Element: Air

intellectauthorityreason

Authority built on reason.

Logical, principled and decisive. Seek fairness.

What the card shows

A crowned king sits on a throne facing forward, holding an upright sword in one hand and gesturing with the other. Clouds and birds fill the sky behind.

Kings turn their suit outward into mastery. Air mastered is judgment, ethics and clear rule. The head-on pose suggests someone who stands behind their words.

Upright

Fair, informed judgment; thinking things through and standing by the result.

Light: intellect, fairness, integrity. Shadow: rigidity, using authority to cut people off.

Love: Clear agreements and fair dealing.

Work: Analysis, decisions made on principle, advising.

To reflect on: What principle do I want my decisions to stand on?

Reversed

Reason becomes rigid or cold, or judgment is clouded.

  • Excess: tyranny of logic.
  • Blocked: inability to decide.
  • Fading: trust in your own judgment shaken.

To reflect on: Am I being fair, or just being firm?
