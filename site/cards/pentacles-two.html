Two of Pentacles

Minor Arcana · Pentacles · Element: Earth

balancejugglingadaptability

Keeping two things in the air.

Juggling priorities with flexibility. Keep your rhythm.

What the card shows

A young man in a tall hat dances while juggling two coins linked by an infinity loop. Behind him ships rise and fall on large waves.

Two is the number of choices and balance. The infinity loop is a flow between two demands, and the waves show that conditions keep changing. He is smiling but must keep moving.

Upright

Balancing competing demands with flexibility.

Light: adaptability, balance, juggling. Shadow: overcommitting, dropping things.

Love: Balancing a relationship with other parts of life.

Work: Handling money and time with limited resources.

To reflect on: What can I stop juggling?

Reversed

Overwhelm, or a balance that falls apart.

  • Blocked: everything feels too much.
  • Excess: taking on too much.
  • Fading: balance slipping.

To reflect on: What is the cost of doing it all?
