Four of Swords

Minor Arcana · Swords · Element: Air

restrecoveryretreat

Rest as a choice.

Pause and recharge. Rest is productive.

What the card shows

A stone figure of a knight lies on a tomb with hands together in prayer. Three swords hang on the wall above and one lies beneath. A stained-glass window shows a figure giving care.

Four is a number of stability, and here it means stillness. The effigy on the tomb is rest and retreat, not death. The stained glass adds a note of quiet healing.

Upright

A needed pause to rest and recover.

Light: rest, recovery, quiet. Shadow: withdrawing too long.

Love: Taking space to heal.

Work: A break, leave or time to think before the next step.

To reflect on: What would resting properly look like?

Reversed

Restlessness, or coming back too soon.

  • Blocked: unable to rest.
  • Releasing: ready to return.
  • Excess: avoiding life.

To reflect on: Am I resting, or hiding?
