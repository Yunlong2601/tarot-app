Eight of Swords

Minor Arcana · Swords · Element: Air

restrictionfearfeeling trapped

Feeling bound by a trap that is partly loose.

You feel stuck, though options may be more open than they seem.

What the card shows

A woman stands blindfolded and bound, surrounded by eight swords in the ground, with shallow water at her feet and a castle on a distant hill.

Eight is movement and mastery. The ties are loose and the swords do not form a closed cage, which is the point: the restriction may be partly in the mind. The shallow water suggests the way out is not far.

Upright

A sense of being stuck that may be more about perception than fact.

Light: awareness of limits, patience, a way out. Shadow: self-imposed limits, helplessness.

Love: Feeling trapped in a pattern.

Work: Feeling powerless in a job or situation.

To reflect on: What would I try if I thought I could?

Reversed

Taking off the blindfold and seeing a way out.

  • Releasing: freedom coming.
  • Blocked: staying in the trap.
  • Excess: victim thinking.

To reflect on: Which of my limits are real, and which are assumed?
