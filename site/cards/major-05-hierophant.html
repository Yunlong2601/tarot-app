The Hierophant

Major Arcana · 5 · Traditional association: Taurus

traditionguidancebelonging

Learning from a shared tradition.

Learning from established wisdom or community can steady you.

What the card shows

A robed figure in a triple crown sits between two grey pillars, one hand raised in blessing. Two crossed keys lie at his feet and two figures kneel in front of him.

The Hierophant is the teacher of established ways. The pillars and keys point to a doorway to knowledge that is passed on in a community. Taurus, his usual sign, is steady and loyal to what has been proven.

Upright

Belonging to a tradition, a group or a teacher, and learning the established way first.

Light: mentorship, shared values, commitment. Shadow: conformity, doing it because it has always been done.

Love: Shared values and rituals that hold people together.

Work: Formal learning, mentors, working inside an institution or a recognised path.

To reflect on: Which tradition or teacher is serving me, and which am I following out of habit?

Reversed

Inherited rules are being questioned, or are stuck in place.

  • Releasing: stepping away from convention.
  • Blocked: unwilling to learn from others.
  • Excess: rules held as dogma.

To reflect on: Whose rules am I living by?
