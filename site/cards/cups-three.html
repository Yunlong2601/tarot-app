Three of Cups

Minor Arcana · Cups · Element: Water

friendshipcelebrationcommunity

Joy shared with others.

Joy shared with others. Enjoy good company.

What the card shows

Three women lift cups together in a celebration, in a garden with harvest fruit around their feet.

Three is the number of growth and community. The raised cups and the harvest show shared gladness in what has grown. Friendship is the theme here more than romance.

Upright

Celebration, friendship and support from a community.

Light: friendship, joy, togetherness. Shadow: overindulgence, gossip.

Love: Warmth from friends and community.

Work: Collaboration and celebrating a team win.

To reflect on: Who do I want to celebrate with?

Reversed

Isolation, or the party turns sour.

  • Blocked: left out or holding back.
  • Excess: too much indulgence.
  • Fading: friendships drifting.

To reflect on: Who has been missing from my circle?
