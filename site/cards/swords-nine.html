Nine of Swords

Minor Arcana · Swords · Element: Air

anxietyworrysleepless nights

Worry in the night.

Worry looms large. Share it with someone you trust.

What the card shows

A person sits up in bed with their face in their hands. Nine swords hang on the dark wall behind. The quilt is covered in roses and astrological signs.

Nine is a number near the end of a cycle, when worry peaks. The swords float on the wall above, not touching the sleeper, which hints that the fears are thoughts more than events.

Upright

Anxiety, worry or guilt that feels heavier at night.

Light: honesty about fear, release. Shadow: catastrophising, sleepless worry.

Love: Anxiety about a relationship.

Work: Stress about outcomes you cannot control.

To reflect on: What worries could I talk about instead of carrying alone?

Reversed

Fears easing, or being buried.

  • Releasing: fear easing.
  • Excess: spiralling.
  • Inward: worry kept hidden.

To reflect on: Who could I tell what is keeping me awake?
