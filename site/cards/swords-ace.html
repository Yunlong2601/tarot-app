Ace of Swords

Minor Arcana · Swords · Element: Air

claritytruthbreakthrough

A clear idea cutting through.

A clear idea cuts through confusion. Speak and think plainly.

What the card shows

A hand from a cloud grips a sword pointing up, with a crown at its tip and olive and palm branches hanging from the crown. Mountains lie below.

Aces are the seed of their suit. Air is thought and truth, so this is clarity and decision. The crown with branches shows both victory and peace, a reminder that a clear mind can serve both.

Upright

A clear insight or a decision that cuts through confusion.

Light: clarity, truth, a breakthrough. Shadow: words used as weapons.

Love: Honest, direct talk that clears the air.

Work: A sharp idea or a clear decision.

To reflect on: What do I see clearly now?

Reversed

Confusion or a truth you avoid.

  • Blocked: unable to think clearly.
  • Excess: harsh words.
  • Inward: truth known but unspoken.

To reflect on: What am I not letting myself admit?
