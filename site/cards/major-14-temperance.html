Temperance

Major Arcana · 14 · Traditional association: Sagittarius

balancepatiencemoderation

Finding the middle path.

Blend opposing needs with patience. Steady, measured steps work best.

What the card shows

An angel with large wings stands with one foot in water and one on land, pouring liquid between two cups. A path winds toward a glow between distant mountains, and irises grow nearby.

Fourteen follows the turning point of the Majors and brings blending and recovery. Pouring between cups is about combining opposites at a steady pace. Sagittarius, the usual sign, adds the long view.

Upright

Blending opposites patiently and keeping a steady pace.

Light: balance, patience, moderation. Shadow: lukewarm avoidance of commitment.

Love: Patience and giving each other room to differ.

Work: A sustainable pace, collaboration, combining skills.

To reflect on: What two things in my life need blending rather than choosing between?

Reversed

Things are out of balance, or pushed to extremes.

  • Excess: overdoing it.
  • Deficit: impatience, wanting results now.
  • Blocked: cannot find a middle way.

To reflect on: Where have I tipped too far to one side?
