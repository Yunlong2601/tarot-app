Knight of Cups

Minor Arcana · Cups · Element: Water

romancecharmidealism

Following the heart, with a romantic eye.

A dreamer who follows the heart. Offers or invitations may come.

What the card shows

A knight in armour with winged helmet and boots rides a calm white horse at a gentle pace, holding a cup in front of him. A river and distant hills lie ahead.

Knights pursue, and in Cups the pursuit is of feeling. The slow horse shows that this is courtship rather than conquest. Wings on the helmet suggest ideals and imagination.

Upright

Pursuing something because it moves you; an offer, an invitation, an ideal.

Light: romance, charm, idealism. Shadow: daydreaming, promises that outrun reality.

Love: A romantic gesture or the wish for one; the gap between the ideal and the person.

Work: Following inspiration; pitching an idea with feeling.

To reflect on: Am I following a person or a picture of them?

Reversed

Charm without substance, or a retreat from feeling.

  • Excess: chasing an ideal that is not real.
  • Blocked: unable to say what you feel.
  • Fading: enthusiasm cooling.

To reflect on: What would change if I acted from what is real, not what I hope?
