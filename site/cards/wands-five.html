Five of Wands

Minor Arcana · Wands · Element: Fire

competitionfrictiondebate

Friction between equally matched energies.

Clashing ideas and rivalry. Competition can sharpen you if kept friendly.

What the card shows

Five young people each swing a staff in what looks like a scuffle on open ground. No one seems to be winning, and no one seems badly hurt.

Five is the number of strain after the stable four. Everyone holds a staff of the same size, so this is competition among equals, more like a rough practice than a war.

Upright

Competing ideas and egos; messy but possibly productive conflict.

Light: competition, vigour, testing ideas. Shadow: pointless arguing, scattered effort.

Love: Disagreements that need a clear direction instead of noise.

Work: Rivalry or brainstorming where nobody leads.

To reflect on: Is this conflict getting me anywhere?

Reversed

Conflict fades, is avoided, or turns into quiet tension.

  • Blocked: avoiding necessary disagreement.
  • Excess: fighting to win.
  • Fading: peace through exhaustion.

To reflect on: What would working together look like here?
