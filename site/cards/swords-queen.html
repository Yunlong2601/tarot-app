Queen of Swords

Minor Arcana · Swords · Element: Air

clarityindependencehonesty

Clear sight, kindly or coldly used.

Clear-eyed and fair. Speak the truth kindly.

What the card shows

A crowned woman sits side-on on a throne, one hand raised and the other holding a sword upright. Clouds and a bird are behind her and the carvings on the throne show butterflies and cherubs.

Queens embody their suit inwardly. Air turned inward becomes clarity and independence. The raised hand is open, and the upright sword shows truth held in readiness, not used to strike.

Upright

Straight, thoughtful honesty grounded in experience.

Light: clarity, independence, perception. Shadow: coldness, cutting remarks, closing off.

Love: Honest communication and clear boundaries.

Work: Seeing through noise to what matters; fair, direct feedback.

To reflect on: What do I see clearly that I have been softening?

Reversed

Clarity turns harsh, or truth is held back.

  • Excess: criticism and cynicism.
  • Blocked: avoiding an honest conversation.
  • Inward: loneliness behind independence.

To reflect on: Is my honesty serving someone, or just protecting me?
