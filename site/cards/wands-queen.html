Queen of Wands

Minor Arcana · Wands · Element: Fire

warmthconfidencecharisma

Warm confidence that lets others be themselves.

Confident, welcoming and driven. Lead by inspiring.

What the card shows

A crowned woman sits on a throne carved with lions, holding a wand in one hand and a sunflower in the other. A black cat sits at her feet.

Queens carry their suit inwardly. Fire turned inward becomes confidence and warmth rather than hurry. Sunflowers face the sun, and lions suggest courage worn lightly.

Upright

Self-assured warmth: knowing who you are and being generous with it.

Light: charisma, loyalty, joy in what you do. Shadow: jealousy, needing to be the centre of attention.

Love: Open, affectionate presence; comfort with being seen.

Work: Leading by enthusiasm; making a place people want to be part of.

To reflect on: Where do I feel most like myself, and am I bringing that in?

Reversed

Confidence wavers, or warmth turns controlling.

  • Blocked: doubting your own worth.
  • Excess: needing admiration.
  • Inward: warmth held back from others.

To reflect on: Am I seeking approval, or sharing what I actually feel?
