King of Wands

Minor Arcana · Wands · Element: Fire

visionleadershipboldness

Vision with the will to lead it.

A natural leader with a clear vision.

What the card shows

A crowned king sits relaxed on a throne decorated with lions and salamanders, holding a flowering wand. His cloak is orange and his gaze goes into the distance.

Kings turn their suit outward into mastery. Fire mastered becomes vision and leadership. The salamanders and lions repeat the fire themes, and the budding wand shows growth under steady hands.

Upright

Leading with energy and a clear picture of where things are going.

Light: inspiration, decisiveness, big-picture thinking. Shadow: domineering, impatience with details or people.

Love: Passion held with maturity: loyal, generous, direct.

Work: Taking charge of a project; setting a direction others will follow.

To reflect on: What am I building toward, and who comes with me?

Reversed

Leadership slips into control, or vision lacks follow-through.

  • Excess: bossiness, running over others.
  • Blocked: a plan that never leaves your head.
  • Fading: energy needing rest.

To reflect on: Am I leading people, or just pushing them?
