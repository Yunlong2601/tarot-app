Three of Pentacles

Minor Arcana · Pentacles · Element: Earth

teamworkcraftskill

Work done well, together.

Skilled collaboration. Good work grows through cooperation.

What the card shows

A craftsman works on a stone carving in a church while two others look on with plans in hand. Three coins are carved in the arch.

Three is the number of growth. This is teamwork: skill, planning and a patron in one room. The church setting shows craftsmanship as something almost sacred.

Upright

Collaboration and skill recognised by others.

Light: teamwork, craft, quality. Shadow: ignoring feedback, working at cross purposes.

Love: Building something together.

Work: A team project, skill-building, getting feedback.

To reflect on: Whose expertise do I need to build this well?

Reversed

Poor teamwork or work without pride.

  • Blocked: collaboration breaking down.
  • Excess: perfectionism.
  • Fading: losing interest in the craft.

To reflect on: Am I working with others, or around them?
