Ace of Pentacles

Minor Arcana · Pentacles · Element: Earth

opportunityprosperityfoundations

A practical seed in your hand.

A seed of opportunity in work or money. Plant it carefully.

What the card shows

A hand from a cloud holds a golden coin marked with a star. Below is a garden with flowers, a stone arch and a path leading to distant mountains.

Aces are the seed of their suit. Earth is work, body and money, so this is a tangible opportunity. The garden and path show that it is a good place to grow something.

Upright

A real opportunity, a solid start in work, health or finances.

Light: opportunity, prosperity, a good foundation. Shadow: a missed chance.

Love: A steady, practical start to a connection.

Work: A new job, offer, or financial chance.

To reflect on: What practical opportunity can I start on?

Reversed

A missed opportunity or a plan with no foundation.

  • Blocked: unable to start.
  • Excess: grabbing too fast.
  • Fading: an opening closing.

To reflect on: What would make this opportunity workable?
