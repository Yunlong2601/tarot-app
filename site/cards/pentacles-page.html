Page of Pentacles

Minor Arcana · Pentacles · Element: Earth

studyambitionpracticality

A patient first step on solid ground.

A hardworking student of life, eager to learn and build.

What the card shows

A young person stands in a green field holding up a single golden coin and studying it closely. Trees and gentle hills lie behind.

Pages begin things, and Pentacles are the physical world. Looking closely at one coin is the picture of careful study. Fertile ground implies that growth is possible.

Upright

Learning a craft or starting a practical goal with care.

Light: diligence, curiosity about the real world, steadiness. Shadow: procrastination, only dreaming.

Love: Slow, sincere interest; showing care through consistency.

Work: A course, an apprenticeship, a first step on a goal.

To reflect on: What one practical step can I take this week?

Reversed

Plans stay dreams, or effort is misdirected.

  • Blocked: waiting for the perfect moment.
  • Excess: perfectionism.
  • Fading: motivation dropping off.

To reflect on: What am I postponing that only needs a start?
