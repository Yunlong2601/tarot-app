Ten of Cups

Minor Arcana · Cups · Element: Water

harmonyfamilyfulfilment

A picture of a happy home.

Emotional fulfilment and a sense of belonging.

What the card shows

A couple stands with arms raised under a rainbow of ten cups, while two children dance beside them. A small house stands in a green landscape.

Ten is the completion of a cycle. Where the Nine is personal, the Ten is shared: a family or community scene. The rainbow arches over everyone like a blessing.

Upright

Emotional fulfilment shared with the people you love.

Light: harmony, belonging, family. Shadow: an ideal that hides real issues.

Love: Deep, lasting closeness and shared values.

Work: A workplace where you feel you belong.

To reflect on: What does a good home or community mean to me?

Reversed

Strain in a family or group, or an ideal out of reach.

  • Blocked: disconnection at home.
  • Excess: pressure to look perfect.
  • Fading: values that no longer match.

To reflect on: What is missing from my picture of belonging?
