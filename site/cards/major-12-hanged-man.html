The Hanged Man

Major Arcana · 12 · Traditional association: Water

pausesurrendernew angle

A pause that shows you something new.

Stopping to see differently can unlock a stuck situation.

What the card shows

A figure hangs upside down by one foot from a living wooden frame, hands behind the back and one leg bent. A halo of light surrounds the calm face.

Twelve closes a full set, and the card is about stopping rather than doing. The hanging is voluntary; the face is peaceful. Water, the usual element, suggests letting go of control and seeing from a new angle.

Upright

Stopping on purpose lets you see the situation from another side.

Light: patience, perspective, letting go. Shadow: stalling, feeling like a martyr.

Love: Pausing to see a situation from the other person's side, or your own.

Work: Waiting for the right moment; a change of viewpoint.

To reflect on: What do I see if I stop trying to fix this?

Reversed

The pause has become delay, or a sacrifice that serves no one.

  • Blocked: stalling.
  • Excess: a martyr role.
  • Releasing: ending a pause and getting ready to move.

To reflect on: Am I waiting for a reason, or avoiding a choice?
