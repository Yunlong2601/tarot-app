Three of Swords

Minor Arcana · Swords · Element: Air

heartachesorrowhonesty

A hurt that deserves to be felt.

Painful truth or sadness. Acknowledge it so it can heal.

What the card shows

A red heart is pierced by three swords against a background of grey clouds and falling rain.

Three is the number of growth, and in Swords it is the painful truth. The picture is stark on purpose: this card names pain without softening it, so that it can be faced.

Upright

Heartache or a painful truth that has to be admitted.

Light: honesty about pain, grief, release. Shadow: dwelling on hurt.

Love: Sadness over a hurt or a betrayal.

Work: Disappointment or a hard conversation.

To reflect on: What hurt am I ready to look at honestly?

Reversed

Recovery beginning, or holding onto old hurt.

  • Releasing: pain easing.
  • Blocked: refusing to feel it.
  • Excess: replaying the wound.

To reflect on: What would healing from this look like?
