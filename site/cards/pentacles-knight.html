Knight of Pentacles

Minor Arcana · Pentacles · Element: Earth

reliabilitypersistenceroutine

Steady effort that gets there in the end.

Dependable and thorough. Slow and steady wins.

What the card shows

A knight in dark armour sits calmly on a heavy, still horse, holding a coin and gazing at it. The field around him is ploughed.

Knights pursue, and in Pentacles they pursue steadily. The horse is the only one in the Knights that stands still, showing patience over speed. The ploughed field shows work already done.

Upright

Reliability and persistence: doing the work and finishing it.

Light: dependability, routine, thoroughness. Shadow: stubbornness, boredom, getting stuck in routine.

Love: Showing love through dependable acts.

Work: Following a long project through; consistent routines.

To reflect on: Where is my steadiness serving me, and where am I just stuck?

Reversed

Routine turns into rut, or progress stalls.

  • Blocked: stuck in the same pattern.
  • Excess: workaholism or rigidity.
  • Fading: losing interest in the long task.

To reflect on: What would a small change to my routine open up?
