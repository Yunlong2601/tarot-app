Ace of Wands

Minor Arcana · Wands · Element: Fire

sparkinspirationnew energy

A spark of drive offered to you.

A burst of enthusiasm and a chance to start something bold.

What the card shows

A hand reaches out of a cloud holding a sturdy wand that sprouts fresh leaves. Below are a river, a castle on a hill and gentle green country.

Aces are the seed of their suit. Fire is will and creative drive, so this is the raw impulse before any plan. The sprouting leaves show life in the wood, and the hand from the cloud is the traditional sign of a gift to take or leave.

Upright

A new burst of energy, inspiration or opportunity, waiting for you to act on it.

Light: inspiration, courage, a fresh start. Shadow: an idea that never leaves the page.

Love: A spark of attraction or a new enthusiasm for the relationship.

Work: A new project, idea or push of motivation.

To reflect on: What do I feel energised to begin right now?

Reversed

The spark is delayed, doubted or spent on the wrong thing.

  • Blocked: no clear outlet for the energy.
  • Excess: starting with fire and no plan.
  • Fading: a burst that runs out quickly.

To reflect on: What is stopping me from acting on what excites me?
