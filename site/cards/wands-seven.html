Seven of Wands

Minor Arcana · Wands · Element: Fire

defenceperseverancestanding firm

Holding your ground.

You hold your ground. Stay firm on what you believe.

What the card shows

A figure on a hilltop holds a wand and fends off six wands rising from below. One foot is in a different shoe from the other.

Seven is the number of tests. The high ground is an advantage, and being outnumbered tests resolve. The mismatched shoes hint at a hasty stand, taken before being fully ready.

Upright

Standing up for a position when challenged.

Light: resilience, boundaries, courage. Shadow: defensiveness, seeing every challenge as an attack.

Love: Defending your needs or a boundary.

Work: Protecting your work or idea under pressure.

To reflect on: What am I defending, and is it worth it?

Reversed

Giving in under pressure, or being worn down.

  • Blocked: backing down too fast.
  • Excess: fighting everything.
  • Fading: running out of energy.

To reflect on: Is this a hill worth holding?
