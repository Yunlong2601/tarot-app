Page of Wands

Minor Arcana · Wands · Element: Fire

curiosityenthusiasmexploration

A spark of curiosity you are allowed to follow.

A spirited newcomer, eager to explore and try ideas.

What the card shows

A young person in a tunic patterned with salamanders stands in a desert holding a tall wand and studying its top. Pyramids sit far behind.

Pages are beginners and messengers. Wands are fire, so this is eager, early-stage drive. The salamanders are a traditional fire symbol, and the barren ground suggests a talent that has not yet been tried.

Upright

Enthusiasm for something new, and the willingness to explore it before being good at it.

Light: curiosity, play, a bold first try. Shadow: scattered interest, dropping things when they get hard.

Love: Fresh excitement about a person or a way of connecting; being upfront about what thrills you.

Work: A new idea or side project; learning by doing.

To reflect on: What am I curious enough about to try badly?

Reversed

Excitement without direction, or a spark that fizzles early.

  • Blocked: fear of looking like a beginner.
  • Excess: starting many things and finishing none.
  • Fading: interest cooling before it has been tested.

To reflect on: Which of my ideas deserves one real week of effort?
