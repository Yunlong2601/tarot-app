King of Cups

Minor Arcana · Cups · Element: Water

emotional balancediplomacycalm

Calm that holds feeling without being ruled by it.

Mature, steady and compassionate. Keep calm under pressure.

What the card shows

A crowned king sits on a throne that floats on rough water, holding a cup in one hand and a sceptre in the other. A fish leaps in the waves and a ship sails behind him.

Kings master their suit outwardly. The throne is steady on turbulent water, which is the whole message: feeling is present but held. The leaping fish shows creativity still alive.

Upright

Emotional maturity: feeling deeply and still choosing how to respond.

Light: diplomacy, balance, generosity. Shadow: emotional distance, suppressing feelings, manipulation.

Love: Steady, kind presence in hard conversations.

Work: Mediating, counselling, leading with calm.

To reflect on: How do I stay steady when the water is rough?

Reversed

Control of feeling hardens into coldness, or composure cracks.

  • Blocked: bottling things up.
  • Excess: moods that run the room.
  • Inward: calm on the surface, storm underneath.

To reflect on: Am I calm, or am I just not showing it?
