Four of Pentacles

Minor Arcana · Pentacles · Element: Earth

securitysavingcontrol

Holding on tightly.

Holding on to what you have. Security is good; watch for clinging.

What the card shows

A crowned figure sits clutching one coin to the chest, with another on the head and two under the feet. A city stands behind.

Four is the number of foundation and, here, rigidity. Every coin is guarded so that nothing can be lost, and nothing can be used. The city behind shows the world he is cut off from.

Upright

Security that tips into clinging, with money, things or control.

Light: security, saving, boundaries. Shadow: greed, fear of loss, control.

Love: Possessiveness or guardedness.

Work: Protecting resources; being too cautious.

To reflect on: What am I holding onto out of fear?

Reversed

Loosening your grip, or spending without thought.

  • Releasing: generosity.
  • Excess: hoarding.
  • Blocked: scarcity fear.

To reflect on: What would I do if I felt safe?
