Seven of Cups

Minor Arcana · Cups · Element: Water

daydreamsoptionsillusion

Many dreams and no clear choice.

Many possibilities and some fantasy. Sort the real from the imagined.

What the card shows

A silhouetted figure stands before seven cups floating in clouds. Each holds something different: a face, a veiled figure, a snake, a castle, jewels, a laurel wreath and a dragon.

Seven is a number of trial. The cups are options, some appealing, some dangerous, all hovering in cloud rather than on solid ground. The figure looks at them and has not yet chosen.

Upright

A wealth of options and fantasies, calling for clear sorting.

Light: imagination, possibility, dreaming. Shadow: illusion, indecision, wishful thinking.

Love: Idealised pictures of love versus the real person.

Work: Too many ideas; choosing one to commit to.

To reflect on: Which of these dreams am I willing to make real?

Reversed

Clearing the fog, or being lost in fantasies.

  • Releasing: seeing clearly at last.
  • Excess: lost in escapism.
  • Blocked: unable to choose.

To reflect on: Which option is real, and which is only attractive?
