The Star

Major Arcana · 17 · Traditional association: Aquarius

hoperenewalcalm

Quiet hope after a hard stretch.

Gentle hope returns after a hard stretch. Rest and trust a slow recovery.

What the card shows

A woman kneels beside a pool, pouring water from two jugs, one onto land and one into the pool. A large eight-pointed star shines above her with seven smaller ones, and a bird perches in a tree behind.

Seventeen comes right after the Tower, so this is calm after upheaval. The two jugs are about giving back to the earth and to yourself. Aquarius, the usual sign, is the water-bearer, linked with hope and openness.

Upright

Gentle renewal and trust coming back after difficulty.

Light: hope, healing, honesty. Shadow: wishing without acting, feeling detached.

Love: Gentleness, starting to trust again.

Work: Renewed motivation; a clearer sense of what you want to work toward.

To reflect on: What helps me feel hopeful, and how can I do more of it?

Reversed

Hope is dimming, or has become vague.

  • Fading: discouragement.
  • Blocked: no light in sight.
  • Excess: wishing without acting.

To reflect on: What small thing could bring back a bit of trust?
