The Chariot

Major Arcana · 7 · Traditional association: Cancer

determinationdrivedirection

Moving forward by holding opposing pulls together.

Willpower and focus move you forward. Hold the reins steady.

What the card shows

An armoured figure stands upright in a chariot under a canopy of stars. A black sphinx and a white sphinx rest in front of it, and the figure holds no reins. A city lies behind.

Seven is the number of tests. The two sphinxes face different ways, so the driver steers by will and focus, not by force. Cancer, the usual sign, gives a protective shell around a determined aim.

Upright

Disciplined drive: you keep moving by holding competing pulls in line.

Light: determination, focus, momentum. Shadow: force without direction, running over others.

Love: Choosing where to put your energy and sticking with it.

Work: Pushing a project through to the end; staying focused under pressure.

To reflect on: What am I steering, and where do I want it to go?

Reversed

Drive is scattered, stalled or turned aggressive.

  • Blocked: stuck without direction.
  • Excess: forcing things, aggression.
  • Inward: pulled two ways at once.

To reflect on: Am I steering, or being dragged?
