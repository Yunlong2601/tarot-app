Seven of Swords

Minor Arcana · Swords · Element: Air

strategysecrecycaution

Acting by stealth.

Think carefully; a sly approach needs honesty about its cost.

What the card shows

A figure tiptoes away from a military camp carrying five swords and looking back, leaving two swords stuck in the ground.

Seven is the number of trials. The sneaking figure is taking a risk alone, and leaving two swords behind shows he could not carry everything. It raises the question of whether the plan is smart or only evasive.

Upright

Strategy, secrecy or avoiding a direct approach.

Light: cleverness, strategy, independence. Shadow: deception, dodging responsibility.

Love: Something not being said; hidden motives.

Work: Taking shortcuts or working around a rule.

To reflect on: Where am I avoiding being direct?

Reversed

Coming clean, or getting caught out.

  • Releasing: confession.
  • Excess: deceit.
  • Blocked: unable to act.

To reflect on: What would honesty cost me here, and what does avoiding it cost?
