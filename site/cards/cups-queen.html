Queen of Cups

Minor Arcana · Cups · Element: Water

empathyintuitioncare

Deep feeling held with care.

Deeply caring and intuitive. Look after yourself too.

What the card shows

A crowned woman sits on a shell-like throne at the water's edge, holding an ornate closed cup with both hands and gazing at it. Small figures decorate the throne.

Queens embody their suit inwardly. The closed, ornate cup shows feelings treasured and not spilled. Being at the shore places her where the conscious and unconscious meet.

Upright

Empathy and intuition used with care, including toward yourself.

Light: compassion, intuition, emotional depth. Shadow: absorbing others' moods, losing your own boundaries.

Love: Listening closely; being available without losing yourself.

Work: Supportive roles, reading what a team needs.

To reflect on: Whose feelings am I carrying that are not mine?

Reversed

Empathy tips into over-giving, or feelings are shut away.

  • Excess: taking on everyone's problems.
  • Blocked: numbing your own feelings.
  • Inward: caring for others but not yourself.

To reflect on: Where do my feelings end and other people's begin?
