The World

Major Arcana · 21 · Traditional association: Saturn

completionwholenessachievement

A cycle complete.

A cycle completes. Take stock of how far you have come.

What the card shows

A dancing figure wrapped in a light scarf holds two wands inside a large laurel wreath. In the four corners are an angel, an eagle, a bull and a lion.

Twenty-one is the last of the Majors, so this is completion. The wreath is a closed loop and the dancing figure moves freely inside it. The four corner figures are tied to the four suits. Saturn, the usual planet, is linked with finishing and limits.

Upright

Completion and wholeness, with the next cycle waiting.

Light: completion, integration, satisfaction. Shadow: reluctance to finish or to move on.

Love: Feeling whole in yourself, and sharing it.

Work: Finishing a project; taking in what you have learned.

To reflect on: What have I finished that deserves to be acknowledged?

Reversed

Something is almost complete but not quite finished.

  • Blocked: the last step is missing.
  • Fading: closure is near.
  • Inward: not quite ready to let go.

To reflect on: What is the last step I keep avoiding?
