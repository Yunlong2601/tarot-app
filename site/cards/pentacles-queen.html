Queen of Pentacles

Minor Arcana · Pentacles · Element: Earth

nurturingpracticalresourceful

Care that is practical and warm.

Warm, practical and generous. Create comfort for yourself and others.

What the card shows

A crowned woman sits in a garden bower of flowers and fruit, holding a coin on her lap and looking at it tenderly. A rabbit is nearby.

Queens embody their suit inwardly. Earth held tenderly is nurture through practical things. The garden and rabbit suggest abundance and fertility of ideas and resources.

Upright

Resourcefulness and nurture: making a home, a budget or a life that works.

Light: generosity, practicality, comfort. Shadow: overworking, neglecting yourself to provide.

Love: Caring through everyday actions and a welcoming home.

Work: Managing resources well; creating a stable environment.

To reflect on: Am I looking after myself with the same care I give others?

Reversed

Support turns into smothering, or self-care is neglected.

  • Excess: taking on too much.
  • Blocked: scarcity worries.
  • Inward: provides for others but not herself.

To reflect on: What do I need to make my own life feel secure?
