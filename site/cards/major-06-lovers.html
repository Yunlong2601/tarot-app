The Lovers

Major Arcana · 6 · Traditional association: Gemini

choiceconnectionalignment

A choice that asks you to be honest about your values.

A meaningful choice that reflects your values, or a bond that asks for honesty.

What the card shows

A man and a woman stand unclothed under a bright sun. An angel spreads its arms above them. Behind the woman is a tree with a snake and behind the man a tree of flames.

Six is the number of exchange and harmony. Gemini, the twins, is the sign of duality and choice. The angel suggests a wider view than either person has alone. Although it is often read as romance, the heart of the card is a choice made in line with what you value.

Upright

A meaningful choice or connection that asks you to act in line with your values.

Light: honesty, harmony, commitment. Shadow: indecision, choosing in order to please.

Love: Honest connection, and whether your actions match what you say you want.

Work: A choice or partnership that fits your values, not only your convenience.

To reflect on: What would I choose if I were fully honest about what matters to me?

Reversed

Choices are delayed, or values and actions are out of tune.

  • Blocked: putting off the decision.
  • Excess: leaning too hard on another person's view.
  • Fading: a sense of drifting apart.

To reflect on: Where am I saying yes while meaning no?
