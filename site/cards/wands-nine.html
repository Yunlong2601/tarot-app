Nine of Wands

Minor Arcana · Wands · Element: Fire

resilienceboundarieslast push

Weary but still standing.

Tired but nearly there. Draw on your resilience.

What the card shows

A bandaged figure leans on a wand and looks warily to one side. Behind him eight wands stand upright like a fence.

Nine is the number near the end of a cycle, when strength is lowest. The bandage shows he has been hurt, and the fence shows he has built defences. He is still on his feet.

Upright

Resilience after a long struggle; one last effort before rest.

Light: persistence, courage, caution. Shadow: stubbornness, distrust after a hard time.

Love: Guarding yourself after hurt.

Work: Nearing the end of a long project, tired but close.

To reflect on: What do I need in order to last the final stretch?

Reversed

Exhaustion, or defences that no longer protect.

  • Blocked: burnout.
  • Excess: paranoia.
  • Fading: giving up near the end.

To reflect on: What can I put down to rest?
