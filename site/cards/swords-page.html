Page of Swords

Minor Arcana · Swords · Element: Air

curiosityvigilanceideas

A sharp mind that wants to find out.

A sharp, inquisitive mind. Ask questions and gather facts.

What the card shows

A young figure stands on a windy hill holding a sword upright with both hands, glancing to the side. Clouds race overhead and trees bend in the wind.

Pages are learners, and Swords are thought and speech. The wind and clouds show restless ideas, and the alert pose is of someone who watches before speaking.

Upright

Curiosity and vigilance: asking questions and noticing details.

Light: alertness, honesty, learning. Shadow: gossip, sharp words used carelessly.

Love: Wanting honest conversation; asking direct questions.

Work: Research, studying, a new way of thinking.

To reflect on: What question have I not asked yet?

Reversed

Words and ideas used to hurt, or thoughts that spin without conclusion.

  • Excess: sarcasm, nit-picking.
  • Blocked: afraid to speak up.
  • Inward: ideas that never get shared.

To reflect on: Am I seeking truth, or just winning an argument?
