Two of Wands

Minor Arcana · Wands · Element: Fire

planningchoicesvision

Standing at the edge of what you know.

You stand at the edge of what is familiar. Decide where to aim.

What the card shows

A figure in a rich robe stands on a castle wall holding a small globe, one hand on a tall wand while another is fixed beside him. He looks out over the sea.

Two is the number of choices and pairs. Holding the globe while looking outward shows planning and wanting more than the home territory. The wall shows he has security but is choosing whether to leave it.

Upright

A decision point between the comfortable and the bigger horizon.

Light: planning, vision, ambition. Shadow: restlessness, over-planning.

Love: Wondering whether to stay settled or grow in a new direction.

Work: Weighing a bigger move against what you have built.

To reflect on: What do I want that is beyond what I have?

Reversed

Fear of leaving the known, or plans that never leave paper.

  • Blocked: staying put out of fear.
  • Excess: planning endlessly.
  • Inward: dreams you have not shared.

To reflect on: Is it caution that keeps me here, or comfort?
