The Tower

Major Arcana · 16 · Traditional association: Mars

upheavalrevelationreset

What was never stable comes down.

A sudden shake-up reveals what was not stable. Clear ground makes room to rebuild.

What the card shows

A tall tower on a crag is struck by lightning. The crown flies off, flames burst from the windows and two figures fall. The sky is dark.

Sixteen is the number of the break. Mars, the usual planet, is the planet of sudden force. The tower is a structure built on a weak base, and the lightning is revelation as much as damage.

Upright

Something built on shaky ground comes down, clearing space for something more honest.

Light: revelation, release, a more honest start. Shadow: shock, fear of loss.

Love: Sudden honesty or a change in a pattern; reacting to news.

Work: Plans that no longer hold; rebuilding a different way.

To reflect on: What was never stable that I have been propping up?

Reversed

The shock is being avoided, or you are in its aftermath.

  • Blocked: resisting something that is already coming apart.
  • Fading: a slow unravelling.
  • Inward: a private crisis.

To reflect on: What small honest change could I make before things have to break?
