Nine of Pentacles

Minor Arcana · Pentacles · Element: Earth

self-reliancecomfortreward

Comfort you built yourself.

Enjoy what your effort has built.

What the card shows

A richly dressed woman stands in a vineyard with a falcon on her gloved hand. A snail crawls at the base of the picture. A grand house is behind.

Nine is near completion and brings private enjoyment of results. The garden is the work of years, and the falcon is trained and calm. The snail suggests slow progress was the route.

Upright

Self-reliance and enjoying what you have earned.

Light: independence, ease, self-sufficiency. Shadow: isolation, valuing comfort over connection.

Love: Being content on your own.

Work: Rewards of long work; financial independence.

To reflect on: What have I built that I can enjoy?

Reversed

Dependence, or comfort that feels empty.

  • Blocked: unable to enjoy it.
  • Excess: living for show.
  • Inward: loneliness behind success.

To reflect on: Does what I have built feel like mine?
