Three of Wands

Minor Arcana · Wands · Element: Fire

foresightprogressexpansion

Having launched, watching what returns.

Early efforts are moving. Look further ahead and keep going.

What the card shows

A figure in a rich cloak stands on a cliff with his back to us, three wands planted around him, looking out over a calm sea where ships sail.

Three is growth beyond the first step. The ships on the water are plans already sent out, and the high place gives perspective. He is waiting and watching rather than rushing.

Upright

Your efforts are in motion; the task now is patience and broader vision.

Light: foresight, expansion, patience. Shadow: impatience, expecting instant returns.

Love: Giving a connection space to develop.

Work: Early results of a project; looking to wider horizons.

To reflect on: What have I set going that I can trust for now?

Reversed

Delays, or plans that do not leave the harbour.

  • Blocked: stalled projects.
  • Excess: expecting too much too fast.
  • Fading: losing sight of the goal.

To reflect on: Am I waiting calmly, or avoiding the next move?
