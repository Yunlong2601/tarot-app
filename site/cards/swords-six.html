Six of Swords

Minor Arcana · Swords · Element: Air

transitionmoving oncalmer waters

Moving toward calmer water.

A slow move away from trouble. Healing is underway.

What the card shows

A ferryman poles a boat carrying a hooded woman and a child, with six swords standing upright in the bow. The water ahead looks calmer.

Six is the number of harmony after trouble. The swords are what they carry with them. The calmer water on the far side suggests a hard passage with something better ahead.

Upright

A transition away from difficulty, carrying lessons with you.

Light: transition, recovery, leaving trouble behind. Shadow: unresolved baggage.

Love: Moving past a rough patch together or apart.

Work: Changing jobs or leaving a hard situation.

To reflect on: What am I leaving behind, and what am I carrying with me?

Reversed

Delayed passage, or trouble that follows you.

  • Blocked: stuck at the shore.
  • Excess: running away.
  • Fading: the transition stalling.

To reflect on: What would I need in order to cross?
