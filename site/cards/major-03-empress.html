The Empress

Major Arcana · 3 · Traditional association: Venus

nurtureabundancecreativity

Growth that comes from care.

Care, comfort and growth are available. Tend what you love and let it flourish.

What the card shows

A woman in a flowing gown patterned with pomegranates sits on cushions in a field of ripe wheat. She wears a crown of stars and holds a sceptre. A heart-shaped shield with the symbol of Venus rests beside her, and a stream and a forest lie behind.

Three is the number of growth and first results. Wheat, water and forest show nature at its fullest. Venus, her usual planet, points to love, pleasure and the senses.

Upright

Nurture, creativity and enjoyment: things grow where they are tended.

Light: generosity, creativity, comfort. Shadow: smothering, overindulgence.

Love: Warmth and care, giving and receiving; learning to let yourself be looked after.

Work: A creative project ripening, caring for a team or a process.

To reflect on: What in my life needs tending rather than pushing?

Reversed

Care is blocked, overdone, or neglected, including care for yourself.

  • Blocked: a creative dry spell.
  • Excess: over-giving or smothering.
  • Fading: your own needs left last.

To reflect on: Am I giving from fullness, or from habit?
