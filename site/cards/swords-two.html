Two of Swords

Minor Arcana · Swords · Element: Air

stalemateindecisionguarded

A decision you are blind to.

A hard choice you are avoiding. Remove the blindfold when ready.

What the card shows

A blindfolded woman sits on a bench holding two crossed swords at chest height. Behind her are calm water, a crescent moon and a rocky shore.

Two is the number of choices. The blindfold shows choosing without looking, and the crossed swords are a stand-off, a defence against feeling. The calm sea shows that there is peace on the surface and tension beneath.

Upright

A stalemate; avoiding a decision by refusing to look.

Light: balance, composure, a pause. Shadow: avoidance, denial.

Love: Holding off from facing a feeling or a choice.

Work: Two options and no way to choose without more information.

To reflect on: What would I see if I took the blindfold off?

Reversed

The choice is forced, or the blindfold comes off.

  • Releasing: seeing the truth.
  • Excess: anxiety of indecision.
  • Blocked: refusing to choose.

To reflect on: What am I protecting myself from by not looking?
