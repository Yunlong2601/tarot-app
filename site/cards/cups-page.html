Page of Cups

Minor Arcana · Cups · Element: Water

sensitivityimaginationopenness

A gentle message from your own feelings.

A tender, creative message. Listen to your feelings.

What the card shows

A young person in a flowered tunic stands by the sea holding a cup, from which a small fish pokes its head. The water behind is calm.

Pages are beginnings, and Cups are feeling and imagination. The fish rising from the cup is a playful picture of an unexpected idea or emotion surfacing. The sea behind is the deeper unconscious.

Upright

A tender, creative impulse; being open to surprise in your inner life.

Light: sensitivity, wonder, imagination. Shadow: moodiness, escaping into daydreams.

Love: A sweet gesture or an honest feeling shared for the first time.

Work: A creative idea that needs room; a gentle beginning.

To reflect on: What small feeling is trying to get my attention?

Reversed

Feelings are held back, or become melodrama.

  • Blocked: afraid to be vulnerable.
  • Excess: oversensitivity.
  • Inward: a creative idea kept secret.

To reflect on: Am I ignoring a quiet feeling because it seems childish?
