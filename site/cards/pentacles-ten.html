Ten of Pentacles

Minor Arcana · Pentacles · Element: Earth

legacyfamilylasting security

A lasting legacy.

Long-term stability, family and shared wealth.

What the card shows

An old man sits with two dogs under an archway. A couple and a child stand nearby, and ten coins are arranged in a pattern above a large estate.

Ten is the completion of a cycle. Where the Nine is personal, the Ten is generational: security that passes on. The arch and the estate show roots and tradition.

Upright

Long-term security, family or community that lasts.

Light: stability, legacy, belonging. Shadow: pressure to conform, being tied to others' expectations.

Love: A long-term, rooted bond.

Work: Lasting business or financial security.

To reflect on: What do I want to leave behind or build to last?

Reversed

Instability in family or finances.

  • Blocked: unstable foundations.
  • Excess: obsession with status.
  • Fading: a legacy dissolving.

To reflect on: What roots do I want to put down?
