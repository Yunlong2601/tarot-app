Six of Pentacles

Minor Arcana · Pentacles · Element: Earth

generositysharingfairness

Giving and receiving fairly.

Giving and receiving in balance. Be fair with resources.

What the card shows

A well-dressed man holds scales in one hand and gives coins to two kneeling figures with the other. A stack of coins is at the side.

Six is the number of harmony. The scales show fairness. The card is about the flow of resources, and it also asks who holds power in the exchange.

Upright

Generosity, fairness and mutual support.

Light: generosity, fairness, support. Shadow: strings attached, uneven power.

Love: Giving and receiving care evenly.

Work: Fair pay, mentoring, sharing resources.

To reflect on: Is the give and take in my life fair?

Reversed

Debt, one-sided giving, or conditions on help.

  • Blocked: unable to give or receive.
  • Excess: giving to control.
  • Fading: support running out.

To reflect on: Who is holding the scales in this situation?
