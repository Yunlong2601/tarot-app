The Moon

Major Arcana · 18 · Traditional association: Pisces

uncertaintydreamsintuition

Things are not yet clear.

Things are not fully clear. Move carefully and notice your fears and instincts.

What the card shows

A full moon with a face shines between two towers. A dog and a wolf howl at it, and a crayfish crawls from a pool onto a winding path that leads into the distance.

Eighteen sits near the end of the journey, where the unconscious is loud. The moon gives light, but not enough to see clearly. Pisces, the usual sign, is dreamy and sensitive.

Upright

Feelings and imagination colour what you see, so take your time and gather more light.

Light: intuition, creativity, imagination. Shadow: fear, projection, confusion.

Love: Unclear feelings, or imagining what another person thinks.

Work: Working with incomplete information; avoiding hasty conclusions.

To reflect on: What am I afraid of that has not actually happened?

Reversed

The fog is lifting, or the confusion is deepening.

  • Fading: things start to become clear.
  • Excess: anxiety spiralling.
  • Inward: a fear kept hidden.

To reflect on: What would look different in daylight?
