The Sun

Major Arcana · 19 · Traditional association: Sun

joyvitalityclarity

Plain, uncomplicated warmth.

Warmth, confidence and openness. Enjoy what is going well.

What the card shows

A child on a white horse holds a red banner under a large sun. Sunflowers rise over a wall behind.

Nineteen is near the end of the journey, and the picture is open and bright, with nothing hidden. The Sun is the card's usual planet: vitality and clarity.

Upright

Warmth, clarity and simple joy.

Light: optimism, vitality, openness. Shadow: overconfidence, ignoring complexity.

Love: Openness and playfulness with the people you trust.

Work: Feeling confident and clear about work you care about.

To reflect on: What brings me plain, uncomplicated joy?

Reversed

The warmth is dimmed or forced.

  • Blocked: clouds in the way.
  • Excess: forced positivity.
  • Fading: tiredness.

To reflect on: Where am I pretending to be brighter than I feel?
