Knight of Swords

Minor Arcana · Swords · Element: Air

ambitionhastedirectness

A direct charge toward an idea.

Driven and quick to act. Watch for bluntness.

What the card shows

A knight in armour gallops fast with a raised sword. Trees bend in a strong wind and clouds stream across the sky.

Knights are action. In Air, action is thought and speech at speed. The horse races into the wind, and nothing in the picture slows it down.

Upright

Decisive, quick thinking; the willingness to say what needs saying.

Light: determination, directness, fast thinking. Shadow: bluntness, haste, not listening.

Love: Saying things plainly; the risk is saying them too sharply.

Work: Pushing a plan through; fast decisions.

To reflect on: Who am I rushing past in my hurry to be right?

Reversed

Haste turns to carelessness, or plans stall in overthinking.

  • Excess: aggression, tactless words.
  • Blocked: scattered thoughts.
  • Fading: motivation running out.

To reflect on: What would I notice if I paused before acting?
