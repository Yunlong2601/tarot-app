Eight of Cups

Minor Arcana · Cups · Element: Water

walking awayseeking moremoving on

Leaving what no longer fills you.

Leaving something that no longer satisfies in search of meaning.

What the card shows

A cloaked figure with a staff walks away from eight stacked cups toward dark hills. A moon appears in the sky. A gap in the stack shows that one cup is missing.

Eight is a turning point. The stacked cups are a neat but incomplete arrangement, and the walker chooses a hard road over staying. The moon suggests a journey made in the dark.

Upright

Walking away from something that looks fine but no longer satisfies.

Light: moving on, honesty, seeking meaning. Shadow: running away, leaving without facing why.

Love: Leaving, or needing to leave, a bond that has run its course.

Work: Quitting a path that no longer fits.

To reflect on: What am I leaving, and what am I going toward?

Reversed

Staying out of fear, or leaving without a plan.

  • Blocked: afraid to leave.
  • Excess: always moving on.
  • Inward: leaving quietly.

To reflect on: Am I leaving for something, or only away from something?
