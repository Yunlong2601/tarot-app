Judgement

Major Arcana · 20 · Traditional association: Fire

reflectionrenewalcalling

A call to look back and begin again.

A moment of honest review. Forgive the past and answer your inner call.

What the card shows

An angel blows a trumpet from the clouds. Below, figures with raised arms rise from open coffins. Snowy mountains stand behind.

Twenty is the number of the awakening before completion. The trumpet is a call, and the rising figures are people answering. The card is about review and renewal, not a verdict handed down.

Upright

A moment of reckoning and renewal: hearing a call to live differently.

Light: awakening, forgiveness, purpose. Shadow: harsh self-judgment.

Love: Forgiveness, and an honest look at a pattern.

Work: Reassessing a path; a sense of calling.

To reflect on: What is calling me that I have been postponing?

Reversed

Doubt, ignoring the call, or being hard on yourself.

  • Blocked: ignoring what you hear.
  • Excess: harsh self-criticism.
  • Inward: a private reckoning.

To reflect on: Can I forgive myself enough to move on?
