Five of Swords

Minor Arcana · Swords · Element: Air

conflicttensionhollow wins

A win that costs something.

A fight that costs more than it gains. Choose your battles.

What the card shows

A man in the foreground holds three swords and looks smug while two others lie on the ground. Two figures walk away with heads down toward a stormy shore.

Five is a number of strain. The man has gathered the swords but at a visible cost: the others walk off defeated, and the sky is stormy. It asks what winning is worth.

Upright

Conflict where the winner may be left with little.

Light: self-interest, strategy, competition. Shadow: pettiness, hollow victories.

Love: An argument won at the expense of the bond.

Work: Competitive workplace tactics.

To reflect on: Is winning this worth what it costs?

Reversed

Making amends, or walking away from a fight.

  • Releasing: reconciliation.
  • Excess: needing to win.
  • Fading: defeat and shame.

To reflect on: Who am I fighting, and why?
