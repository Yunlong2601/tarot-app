Five of Cups

Minor Arcana · Cups · Element: Water

lossregretgrief

Grief that hides what remains.

Focus on what is lost. Remember that some cups still stand.

What the card shows

A cloaked figure in black faces three spilled cups. Two cups still stand upright behind him. A river, a bridge and a house are in the distance.

Five is a number of loss and strain. The spilled cups are what has gone, and the standing cups are what remains. The turned back is the point: he has not yet looked around.

Upright

Mourning what was lost, with something still left to turn toward.

Light: grief, regret, honest feeling. Shadow: dwelling on loss, bitterness.

Love: Sadness over a relationship that changed.

Work: Disappointment with a result.

To reflect on: What remains that I have not looked at yet?

Reversed

Moving on, forgiveness, or being stuck in regret.

  • Releasing: turning to what is left.
  • Blocked: refusing to let go.
  • Inward: grief unspoken.

To reflect on: What am I ready to stop mourning?
