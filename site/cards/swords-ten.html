Ten of Swords

Minor Arcana · Swords · Element: Air

endingsexhaustionrock bottom

The end of something painful.

A painful chapter ends. The only direction now is forward.

What the card shows

A figure lies face down with ten swords in their back against a black sky. On the horizon, dawn lights a calm sea.

Ten is the end of a cycle. The picture is dramatic on purpose, but the dawn on the horizon means that the worst is over: there is nowhere further down to go. It is exaggerated pain, not a literal omen.

Upright

A painful ending that clears the way for a new start.

Light: release, an ending, a fresh start. Shadow: dramatising, clinging to being the victim.

Love: Closing a painful chapter.

Work: A project or job ends; relief in being done.

To reflect on: What is over that I can finally let be over?

Reversed

Recovery slowly beginning, or refusing to let go of the pain.

  • Releasing: dawn after the worst.
  • Excess: dramatising.
  • Blocked: unwilling to end it.

To reflect on: What would starting over look like?
