Strength

Major Arcana · 8 · Traditional association: Leo

couragepatiencecompassion

Courage that is gentle.

Quiet courage and gentleness handle what force cannot.

What the card shows

A woman in white, with an infinity sign above her head, calmly closes the mouth of a lion. She wears a garland of flowers and mountains rise behind.

In this deck Strength is numbered eight (Justice is eleven). Eight is the number of steady effort. The picture shows strength as patience rather than force: the lion is not beaten but befriended. Leo, her usual sign, is the lion.

Upright

Quiet courage: meeting a strong feeling with patience and kindness instead of a fight.

Light: patience, resilience, self-compassion. Shadow: self-doubt, pushing feelings down.

Love: Staying steady and gentle when emotions run high.

Work: Keeping calm under pressure; leading by example.

To reflect on: What would patience look like here?

Reversed

Strength is drained, or force takes the place of gentleness.

  • Fading: running low on energy.
  • Blocked: self-doubt.
  • Excess: trying to tame a feeling by force.

To reflect on: What am I trying to force that needs gentleness?
