The Hermit

Major Arcana · 9 · Traditional association: Virgo

solitudereflectioninner guidance

Stepping back to find your own answer.

Step back to think. Time alone can show you what matters.

What the card shows

An old figure in a grey cloak stands on a snowy peak. One hand holds a lantern with a six-pointed star inside and the other a staff.

Nine is the number of near-completion and solitude. The lantern lights only the next few steps, which is how inner guidance often works. Virgo, the usual sign, is careful and discerning.

Upright

Time apart to hear your own thoughts and decide what matters.

Light: wisdom, focus, chosen solitude. Shadow: isolation, avoiding people or choices.

Love: Time alone to understand what you want; sharing it when ready.

Work: Deep focus, research, finding a mentor or becoming one.

To reflect on: What do I hear when everything gets quiet?

Reversed

Solitude turns into isolation, or you cannot be alone with your thoughts.

  • Excess: withdrawing too far.
  • Blocked: restless, unable to be still.
  • Fading: coming back toward other people.

To reflect on: Is this solitude restoring me or hiding me?
