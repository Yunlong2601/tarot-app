Seven of Pentacles

Minor Arcana · Pentacles · Element: Earth

patienceassessmentlong view

Pausing to see what grew.

Pause to review your efforts. Growth takes time.

What the card shows

A farmer leans on his hoe and looks at a bush bearing seven coins. A work of effort has already been done.

Seven is the number of trial. After planting and tending, the farmer pauses and looks. The coins have grown but are not yet harvested, so the question is whether to keep waiting.

Upright

A pause to assess the results of long effort.

Light: patience, assessment, long-term thinking. Shadow: impatience, doubting your effort.

Love: Taking stock of what a long investment has produced.

Work: Reviewing progress and deciding on the next step.

To reflect on: Is the growth I see worth continuing for?

Reversed

Frustration at slow progress, or giving up too early.

  • Blocked: impatience.
  • Excess: overworking.
  • Fading: losing faith in the work.

To reflect on: What have I actually built so far?
