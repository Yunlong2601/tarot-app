Nine of Cups

Minor Arcana · Cups · Element: Water

contentmentsatisfactiongratitude

Contentment you can enjoy.

A sense of fulfilment. Appreciate what you have.

What the card shows

A man in a red hat sits on a bench with arms crossed and a satisfied look. Behind him on a curved table stand nine cups under a blue cloth.

Nine is near the end of a cycle, when results can be enjoyed. The cups are in a neat display, and his posture is relaxed. It is commonly called the wish card, but it may also point to private satisfaction.

Upright

Satisfaction with what you have worked for.

Light: contentment, gratitude, enjoyment. Shadow: complacency, showing off.

Love: Feeling content and secure.

Work: Reaching a personal goal you are glad of.

To reflect on: What do I already have that I am glad of?

Reversed

Dissatisfaction despite having what you wanted.

  • Blocked: nothing feels enough.
  • Excess: indulgence.
  • Inward: satisfaction you do not share.

To reflect on: What would feel like enough?
