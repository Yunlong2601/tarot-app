King of Pentacles

Minor Arcana · Pentacles · Element: Earth

abundancestabilityleadership

Stability built over time.

Established, generous and secure. Lead steadily.

What the card shows

A crowned king sits on a throne carved with bulls and decorated with vines, holding a coin and a sceptre. A castle and a garden are behind him.

Kings turn their suit outward into mastery. Earth mastered is lasting security. Bulls and vines suggest strength and abundance that have been cultivated.

Upright

Prosperity through patience, discipline and fair dealing.

Light: reliability, generosity, long-term thinking. Shadow: materialism, resisting change.

Love: Providing security and commitment.

Work: Building something lasting; sound financial habits.

To reflect on: What am I building that will still stand in five years?

Reversed

Security hardens into possessiveness, or resources are mismanaged.

  • Excess: valuing things over people.
  • Blocked: fear of loss.
  • Fading: neglected foundations.

To reflect on: What does enough look like for me?
