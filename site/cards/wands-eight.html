Eight of Wands

Minor Arcana · Wands · Element: Fire

speedmovementmessages

Things moving fast.

Things move fast. Act promptly and keep communication open.

What the card shows

Eight wands fly diagonally across the sky over a quiet landscape with a river. No figure appears in the scene.

Eight is movement and mastery of a field. With no people shown, this is momentum itself: things happening faster than anyone steers them, like messages arriving.

Upright

Quick progress, news or events that carry you along.

Light: speed, news, momentum. Shadow: rushing, scattered attention.

Love: Quick communication or developments.

Work: Fast progress, messages, travel.

To reflect on: What am I ready to let move quickly?

Reversed

Delays, crossed wires, or things moving faster than you can manage.

  • Blocked: delays and waiting.
  • Excess: haste and mistakes.
  • Fading: momentum lost.

To reflect on: Where do I need to slow down to catch up?
