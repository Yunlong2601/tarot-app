Six of Cups

Minor Arcana · Cups · Element: Water

nostalgiainnocencekindness

Warmth from the past.

Memories and gentle generosity. Revisit the past with warmth.

What the card shows

In a courtyard of a village, a child hands a cup full of flowers to a smaller child. Other cups with flowers sit around, with a guard in the background.

Six is the number of harmony. The childhood scene points to innocence and old bonds, with flowers as a gift. Nostalgia is the theme, along with simple kindness.

Upright

Memory, simple kindness and old connections.

Light: nostalgia, kindness, innocence. Shadow: living in the past.

Love: Reuniting with an old friend, or sentimental tenderness.

Work: Returning to what you used to love doing.

To reflect on: What from my past is worth bringing forward?

Reversed

Stuck in memories, or letting go of the past.

  • Blocked: unable to leave the past.
  • Releasing: moving on from old patterns.
  • Excess: idealising what was.

To reflect on: Am I remembering the past, or hiding in it?
