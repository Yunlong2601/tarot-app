Four of Cups

Minor Arcana · Cups · Element: Water

apathycontemplationreevaluation

Not seeing what is being offered.

Boredom or withdrawal. Ask what you truly want.

What the card shows

A figure sits under a tree with arms and legs crossed, looking at three cups on the ground. A hand from a cloud offers a fourth cup, which he does not seem to notice.

Four is a pause. The three cups hold his attention while a new one is offered. The folded arms show withdrawal, and the cloud-hand shows that something is on offer if he looks up.

Upright

A pause for reflection, and the question of whether boredom is hiding an opportunity.

Light: reflection, contemplation, reassessment. Shadow: apathy, missing what is offered.

Love: Feeling flat in a bond, or ignoring a gesture of care.

Work: Boredom with what you have; space to rethink.

To reflect on: What am I overlooking because I am looking at what I lack?

Reversed

Waking up to something you ignored, or sinking deeper into apathy.

  • Releasing: seeing the offer at last.
  • Blocked: stuck in withdrawal.
  • Excess: rejecting everything.

To reflect on: What might I accept if I looked up?
