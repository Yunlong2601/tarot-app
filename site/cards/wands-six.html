Six of Wands

Minor Arcana · Wands · Element: Fire

recognitionprogressconfidence

Recognition after effort.

Public success or a boost of confidence for your efforts.

What the card shows

A rider on a decorated horse wears a laurel wreath and carries a wand with a wreath on top. Crowds with staves walk alongside.

Six is the number of harmony after trouble. The wreath is an old symbol of victory, and the crowd shows public acknowledgement. The rider is seen by others, which is the point of the card.

Upright

Success that others can see, and the confidence that comes with it.

Light: victory, recognition, pride. Shadow: ego, needing applause.

Love: Feeling proud of a shared achievement.

Work: A win, a promotion, finishing something visible.

To reflect on: What have I done that I could let myself be proud of?

Reversed

Success is delayed or hollow, or recognition feels out of reach.

  • Blocked: a win that does not land.
  • Excess: pride.
  • Fading: effort unnoticed.

To reflect on: Whose approval am I waiting for?
