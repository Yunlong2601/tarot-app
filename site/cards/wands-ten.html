Ten of Wands

Minor Arcana · Wands · Element: Fire

burdenresponsibilityoverload

Carrying more than you were meant to.

You are carrying a lot. Consider what you can set down.

What the card shows

A figure bends forward under the weight of ten bundled wands, heading toward a village in the distance.

Ten is the end of a cycle, and here it is overload. The wands are all gathered into one burden, and the village shows that the goal is close. The question is whether it all has to be carried alone.

Upright

Responsibility and effort at their limit, and a sign to share the load.

Light: dedication, hard work, nearly there. Shadow: overwork, taking on too much.

Love: Carrying everything alone in a relationship.

Work: Overloaded schedule, burnout, a project near its end.

To reflect on: What can I hand over or put down?

Reversed

Dropping the burden, or collapsing under it.

  • Releasing: finally sharing the load.
  • Excess: refusing help.
  • Blocked: unable to put anything down.

To reflect on: What am I carrying that was never mine?
