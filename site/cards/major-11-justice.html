Justice

Major Arcana · 11 · Traditional association: Libra

fairnesstruthaccountability

An honest look at the facts, and at your part in them.

Weigh the facts honestly. Clear, fair choices bring balance.

What the card shows

A crowned figure in a red robe sits between two pillars. One hand holds an upright sword and the other a pair of scales. A purple veil hangs behind.

In this deck Justice is numbered eleven. The scales weigh, the sword cuts through, and the figure is seated, so the card is about calm judgment and cause and effect. Libra, the usual sign, is the sign of the scales.

Upright

Seeing a situation fairly and owning your choices.

Light: fairness, clarity, accountability. Shadow: harsh judgment, rigidity.

Love: An honest conversation about what each person is doing.

Work: Decisions based on honest facts, treating people fairly.

To reflect on: What would a fair account of this situation include?

Reversed

Bias, avoiding consequences, or judging yourself too harshly.

  • Blocked: avoiding accountability.
  • Excess: harsh judging of others.
  • Inward: the scales are unfair to yourself.

To reflect on: Am I being fair to myself?
