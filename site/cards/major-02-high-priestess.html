The High Priestess

Major Arcana · 2 · Traditional association: Moon

intuitionstillnessthe unspoken

What you sense before you can explain it.

Quiet reflection brings answers that noise cannot. Trust what you sense beneath the surface.

What the card shows

A woman in blue robes sits between a black pillar and a white pillar marked with the letters B and J. A crescent moon rests at her feet and a scroll is half hidden in her lap. Behind her hangs a curtain patterned with pomegranates.

Two is the number of pairs and thresholds. The pillars mark a doorway between opposites, and the curtain hides what lies beyond it. The Moon, her usual association, is the traditional symbol of intuition and what is not yet visible.

Upright

Stillness before deciding: listening to what is unspoken, in the situation and in you.

Light: intuition, patience, discretion. Shadow: secrecy, holding back too long.

Love: Noticing what is left unsaid between people, and what you have not admitted to yourself.

Work: Gathering information before acting; trusting a quiet read of the room.

To reflect on: What do I already sense but have not said out loud?

Reversed

Intuition is drowned out, or secrets are kept too tightly.

  • Blocked: noise and busyness over the inner voice.
  • Inward: feelings hidden even from yourself.
  • Excess: overthinking, reading too much into small signs.

To reflect on: When did I last go quiet enough to hear what I think?
