Tarot birth card calculator

A traditional numerology method: add your birth month, day and year, then add the digits until you reach 22 or less. That number is a Major Arcana card (22 is The Fool). A two-digit result also gives the card for the sum of its digits. Worked out in your browser; nothing is sent.
