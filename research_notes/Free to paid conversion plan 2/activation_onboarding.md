# First-session activation and onboarding personalisation (tarot / astrology / spiritual / AI-chat subscription apps)

Scope note: Public, app-specific conversion numbers for Nebula, Co-Star, The Pattern, Hint, Moonly, Replika and Character AI are essentially **not published**. The hard numbers below come from cross-category benchmarks (RevenueCat, Adapty), vendor case studies (RevenueCat/Superwall/Adapty customers) and one controlled field experiment (Headspace x Irrational Labs x Purchasely). Vendor-published claims are flagged as such. Research conducted Oct 2026.

## Q1. What onboarding patterns (Nebula, Co-Star, The Pattern, Hint, Moonly, Replika, Character AI, Calm, Headspace) raise conversion, with numbers where published?

### Takeaway
The first session is where the money is made: about 80% or more of trials and roughly 90% of first purchases happen on install day. The pattern that wins across categories is a short quiz with visible "tailoring" (intent, goal, birth data). It is followed by a personalised result or plan and then a paywall shown inside onboarding. The only controlled experiment found, Headspace's, shows that a quiz raises the first core action mainly because users *feel* the experience was tailored ("perceived fit"), not because the matching is better. No astrology, tarot or AI-companion app has published its own conversion figures.

### Cited Findings
**Cross-category benchmarks (first session is decisive)**
- 82% of trial starts happen on install day (Day 0), and Day 0 exceeds 80% in every category. Commentators in the report put it at 80–90% — [RevenueCat State of Subscription Apps 2025](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2025/)
- Median download-to-trial is 6.2%. Top-10% apps reach 20.3%, more than 3x the median — [RevenueCat SOSA 2025](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2025/)
- Hard paywall median download-to-paid is 12.11% vs 2.18% for freemium. Refund rate is 5.8% (hard) vs 3.4% (freemium). Median 1-year retention on monthly plans is 12.8% (hard) vs 9.3% (freemium) — [RevenueCat SOSA 2025](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2025/)
- In Adapty's data, 93% of first purchases happen within 24h of install and generate 89% of first-90-day revenue. 53% of subscription revenue comes from subscriptions started in the first session (Adapty data Oct 2025–Sep 2026, 78k paywalls, 30k+ apps, vendor-published) — [Adapty State of Paywalls 2026](https://adapty.io/state-of-paywalls/report/)
- In AI apps, revenue per install at day 60 is a median $0.63, double the overall median of $0.31 — [RevenueCat SOSA 2025](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2025/)
- Hybrid monetisation (subscriptions plus consumables or lifetime) is used by 35%+ of apps, including 39.4% of Social & Lifestyle apps. Nearly 20% of Social & Lifestyle apps mix subscriptions with consumables. This makes ShenTarot's credits-plus-subscription model category-normal — [RevenueCat SOSA 2025](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2025/)

**Headspace: the only controlled personalisation experiment found**
- Headspace, Irrational Labs and Purchasely tested five onboarding variants on new free-trial users. Course starts were 31.25% in the control vs 62.97% in the "perceived fit" variant (about 2x). The quiz version beat "default to basics, no questions" by 7.6 percentage points, even though everyone was given the same Basics course — [Irrational Labs case study](https://irrationallabs.com/case-studies/headspace-doubled-course-starts/)
- Actually routing users to different courses did not beat Basics; the matching logic over-weighted sleep while stress and anxiety were the most common needs. A precommitment variant (plan when and how you'll meditate) raised app opens by 7.5% and unique open days by 4%. No variant significantly changed active meditation days. Paid conversion was **not reported**. Publication date is unclear and appears recent (2025–26) — [Irrational Labs](https://irrationallabs.com/case-studies/headspace-doubled-course-starts/)
- Headspace's onboarding asks about meditation experience and "what brings you" to the app, then guides the user's choice of session length (older teardown, likely pre-2023) — [Appcues GoodUX](https://goodux.appcues.com/blog/headspaces-mindful-onboarding-sequence)

**Calm**
- No published experiment on Calm's onboarding goal question was found. The closest Calm data is an email-cadence test: compressing 8 onboarding emails from 27 days to 15 raised engagement. The "4x revenue" headline has no derivation — [Iterable customer story](https://iterable.com/customers/calm)

**Astrology and spiritual apps (qualitative only)**
- Nebula: a teardown captured 174 screens, with the paywall about 44 seconds into the flow and repeated "Unlock Pro" prompts. The most common model in the category is "Free Trial – Soft Paywall", and astrology-adjacent categories average about 6–8 onboarding steps (ScreensDesign, third-party revenue estimate) — [ScreensDesign: Nebula](https://screensdesign.com/apps/nebula-horoscope-astrology/), [ScreensDesign moon-phase category](https://screensdesign.com/categories/moon-phase-astrology-apps/)
- Nebula's paywall library listing shows weekly $7.99, annual $39.99 and a $9.99 chat credit, i.e. a hybrid subscription-plus-credits model like ShenTarot's — [Adapty paywall library: Nebula](https://adapty.io/paywall-library/nebula-horoscope-astrology/)
- **Warning:** user reports describe Nebula's quiz-to-paywall flow (birth time, sign, "personal vibes") followed by unexpected weekly billing, and "$1 reading then $39 charge" complaints. Some of the conversion likely comes from unclear disclosure, a refund and review risk that should not be copied — [Medium user review](https://medium.com/@emeldawheary61/the-astrology-app-that-quietly-emptied-my-wallet-nebula-review-697ef2defb02), [Apple Community thread](https://discussions-kr-prz.apple.com/thread/256021474)
- Co-Star: onboarding collects birth date, time and place to compute a chart. The product's retention engine is daily transit push notifications in a distinctive blunt voice. In 2018 it had about 3M downloads, and the company estimated about 20% of young Americans had downloaded it. These figures are pre-2023 — flag older — [Wikipedia: Co–Star](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Co%E2%80%93Star), [Inverse 2018](https://inverse.com/article/54991-costar-astrology-app-how-it-works)
- No teardown or numbers were found for The Pattern, Hint, Moonly or Labyrinthos (see Gaps).

**AI companion and chat apps**
- Replika's official first run: pick gender and 3D avatar, customise appearance, name the companion, then a first daily login reward. Only subscribers can change the avatar after setup — [Replika help: How do I begin](https://help.replika.com/hc/en-us/articles/115001095892-How-do-I-begin-Replika), [Replika help: avatar](https://help.replika.com/hc/en-us/articles/360046050652-How-do-I-change-my-Replika-s-avatar)
- A third-party UX teardown says Replika's onboarding quiz covers emotional needs and relationship goals, ends in a subscription paywall, and offers no free trial (single analyst, unverified) — [ScreensDesign: Replika](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/replika-ai-friend)
- Replika says the companion "gets to know" the user in its early levels, a learning curve that builds attachment over time — [Replika help: levels](https://help.replika.com/hc/en-us/articles/37208462290829-How-do-Replika-levels-work)
- Character AI: browse and chat before sign-up, so the first value comes before any account. Characters send "away messages" to re-engage users. The upgrade prompt comes after usage (c.ai+ $9.99/mo). Sources are lower confidence — [William Fu teardown](https://williamfu.substack.com/p/character-ai-product-teardown), [Twofold funnel review (unverified provenance)](https://funnel-researcher-dashboard.internal.trytwofold.com/screenshots/character-ai)

**Why long quiz funnels work (web2app evidence, 2025–26)**
- Noom runs up to 113 screens and builds "progressive commitment": sensitive questions get context, expectations are repeated, and the paywall comes only after a large time investment — [RevenueCat: web-to-app onboarding funnel](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/web-to-app-onboarding-funnel.md)
- Funnelfox describes how funnels make the user feel the tailoring: progress bars, profile summaries, "building your plan" screens, with mini-insights and social proof between questions to prevent fatigue. Nebula is one of the funnels it analyses — [Funnelfox 2026](https://blog.funnelfox.com/web2app-funnel-patterns-2026/)
- Counterpoint: Funnelfox also argues that length is overrated and that engagement and trust matter more — [Funnelfox](https://blog.funnelfox.com/build-web-onboarding-that-converts-in-6-steps/)

### Inferences
- For ShenTarot the "aha" is almost certainly **the first reading that names the user's own question and situation**. The Headspace result implies that the *visible act* of asking and then reflecting the user's answers back drives action, even with modest real personalisation.
- Astrology leaders (Nebula, Co-Star) use birth data as both a personalisation input and an investment or commitment device. For tarot, the natural equivalent is "what's on your mind / which area of life" plus an optional birth date (zodiac sign) to add astrological flavour to readings.
- The Nebula complaints show that tactics like a $1 intro followed by a large charge raise refunds and erode trust. ShenTarot's annual plan at $1.99 for the first month and then $39.99 needs very clear disclosure.

### Gaps
- No published conversion or retention data from Nebula, Co-Star, The Pattern, Hint, Moonly, Labyrinthos, Replika or Character AI. Their numbers are private; ScreensDesign revenue figures are third-party estimates.
- No found study isolating quiz length vs conversion for astrology or tarot specifically.
- The Headspace experiment did not report trial-to-paid.

## Q2. Where should the paywall appear in onboarding (before or after first value), and what do experiments show?

### Takeaway
The evidence strongly favours **showing a paywall during the first session, inside or at the end of onboarding**. Onboarding paywalls convert about 3.7x better per view than later ones (Adapty). Several case studies report 2–5x lifts from moving the paywall earlier. Most top apps place it in the last third of onboarding. When the product can deliver value in seconds (PhotoRoom, and plausibly a one-card tarot reading), the best-practice placement is **immediately after the aha moment**, still within the first session. No controlled, published before-vs-after-value test exists. Every case study confounds placement with other changes.

### Cited Findings
- Median paywall view-to-paid is 0.85% for onboarding paywalls vs 0.23% for later paywalls (about 3.7x). Revenue per 1,000 views is $170 vs $40. About 70% of paywall views happen after the first session but yield only 38% of revenue. For Lifestyle and Utilities, 48% of revenue starts after the first session, so later paywalls matter more there than average (vendor data) — [Adapty State of Paywalls 2026](https://adapty.io/state-of-paywalls/report/)
- Greg (plant care) moved its paywall from "after the 5th plant" into onboarding for all users. Sign-up-to-trial went from 3% to 15% (5x) and trials rose about 400%. Greg also switched from usage-based to feature-based gating at the same time. Rootd (mental wellness) moved the paywall early in onboarding and reported 5x revenue. PhotoRoom shows the paywall right after the aha moment (background removal) during onboarding, with no figures given (Nov 2023, updated June 2024) — [RevenueCat: paywall placement](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/paywall-placement/)
- FitnessAI moved its paywall before onboarding and added a video. Paywall exposure rose 50%, install-to-trial roughly doubled, and trial-to-paid held steady, so revenue about doubled. The video change was made simultaneously — [RevenueCat blog search summary / Jake Mor](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/paywall-placement.md)
- Jake Mor (Superwall) says the winning combination tends to be a paywall on app open, before any locked feature, both before and after onboarding. No data accompanies this — [RevenueCat: paywall placement](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/paywall-placement/)
- RevenueCat reports that trial opt-in correlates with the number of paywall views — [RevenueCat SOSA 2025](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2025/)
- In a sample of 40 onboarding flows, 77.5% place the first paywall in the final third, 27.5% on the final step, and none open on a paywall; the median is step 11 of 17. This shows practice, not outcomes — [Lazyweb research](https://www.lazyweb.com/research/how-far-into-onboarding-do-apps-place-the-paywall)
- Trial-to-paid medians are 39.9% for Health & Fitness (top 10%: 68.3%), 45.7% for 17–32 day trials vs about 26.8% for shorter ones, and 47.8% for lower-priced apps vs 28.4% for high-priced. 3-day and 7-day trials see the most Day 0 and Day 1 cancellations — [RevenueCat SOSA 2025](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2025/)
- On trial length the two vendors disagree. RevenueCat finds 52% of 2024 trials were 5–9 days ([SOSA 2025](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2025/)), while Adapty finds 79% of trial *starts* were 1–3 days ([State of Paywalls 2026](https://adapty.io/state-of-paywalls/report/)). The samples and definitions differ, so neither should be treated as definitive.
- Nearly 30% of annual subscriptions are cancelled in the first month, and first-renewal churn is 15–40% for monthly plans — [RevenueCat SOSA 2025](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2025/)
- Superwall testimonial: targeting unsubscribed users with an additional trial offer raised trial starts by 240% and proceeds per user by 97% (self-reported, unverified) — [Superwall marketers page](https://superwall.com/solutions/marketers)
- Industry caution: reshuffling onboarding screens and paywall placement can have second-order effects beyond the test metric — [Growth Gems](https://growthgems.substack.com/p/10-gems-on-monetization-prioritizing)

**App Store rules (verified against Apple's guidelines page, Oct 2026)**
- 3.1.2(c): "Before asking a customer to subscribe, you should clearly describe what the user will get for the price." — [App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)
- 3.1.2(a): auto-renewable subscriptions must "provide ongoing value", last at least seven days, and work across the user's devices. Free trials are configured through App Store Connect — [App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)
- 3.1.1: purchased credits "may not expire", and a restore mechanism is required for restorable purchases. This applies to ShenTarot's credit packs, and probably to welcome credits only if they are purchased; the guideline wording covers purchased credits — [App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)
- 4.3(b): fortune-telling apps are listed as a saturated category, and new submissions must offer "a meaningfully different or improved experience". Astrology is not named — [App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)

### Inferences
- ShenTarot's free first premium spread is a value-first design. The data suggests the paywall should appear **in the first session, right after that first reading lands**. A user who closes the app before seeing a paywall is far less likely to ever convert (about 90% of first purchases happen on Day 0).
- A reasonable test design: (A) the current flow vs (B) a soft paywall at the end of onboarding, before the first reading, with the free reading still available on dismiss vs (C) a paywall right after the free premium reading, with the reading's last insight or a follow-up question gated. Given PhotoRoom-style "aha then paywall" and Adapty's onboarding vs later gap, C is the most promising for an app whose value can be shown in under a minute.
- The $1.99 first month on the annual plan is an intro offer. Disclose the renewal price prominently next to the CTA to stay within 3.1.2(c) and avoid Nebula-style complaints. Adapty notes that 74% of trial paywalls place reassurance copy near the CTA.

### Gaps
- No published controlled test of "paywall before first value vs after first value" with trial-to-paid and refunds measured. The case studies are confounded.
- No benchmarks specific to astrology, tarot or AI-chat paywalls; Adapty's 8 categories exclude them.

## Q3. How do personalisation questions get reused afterwards (in readings, notifications) to raise perceived value?

### Takeaway
Leading apps reuse onboarding answers in three places: **the paywall** (routing and headlines), **the core output** (readings or plans that cite the user's goal or chart), and **re-engagement** (daily personalised push, companion "away messages"). Personalised paywalls and onboarding show 13–35% ARPU lifts in vendor tests. Yet only 2% of paywall headlines use what the app knows about the user, which leaves an easy opening.

### Cited Findings
- Only 1 in 4 apps personalise paywalls, but 69% of the top 10% (by revenue per view, mid-size apps) do vs 42% of the rest. Only 15% target by user attributes, and only **2% of paywall headlines use what the app knows about the user**. Personalised onboarding increased ARPU by 13–35% in Adapty's tests (vendor) — [Adapty State of Paywalls 2026](https://adapty.io/state-of-paywalls/report/)
- Adapty's AI Studio case study: users on a personalised path converted to paid at 4.7% vs 2.2% on the default path (vendor case study) — [Adapty: AI Studio case study](https://adapty.io/case-studies/ai-studio/)
- Superwall's documented pattern: onboarding Flow answers (goals, experience, preferences) are saved as user attributes and used as audience filters that route each user to a different paywall — [Superwall: personalize paywalls based on onboarding](https://superwall.dev/solutions/personalize-paywalls-based-on-ads-onboarding-more)
- Co-Star turns birth data into daily transit push notifications and interpretations. Personalised push is the core habit loop (pre-2023 source) — [Wikipedia: Co–Star](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Co%E2%80%93Star)
- Character AI characters send "away messages" while the user is absent. Companion-app guidance stresses memory across sessions, so the fifth visit opens differently from the first — [William Fu teardown](https://williamfu.substack.com/p/character-ai-product-teardown), [Meganova blog](https://blog.meganova.ai/how-to-build-an-ai-companion-character-that-keeps-users-coming-back)
- Replika: a companion that "learns" the user over levels and accumulates personal context — [Replika help: levels](https://help.replika.com/hc/en-us/articles/37208462290829-How-do-Replika-levels-work)
- MoEngage 2024: behaviour-based personalised notifications gave 2.17x higher conversions and 1.79x higher CTR on iOS push (vendor benchmark; measures conversions and clicks, not retention) — [MoEngage Customer Engagement Benchmarks 2024](https://www.moengage.com/benchmarks/customer-engagement-2024/)
- Over-frequent, generic push increased churn in a pregnancy-tracker case study — [nGrow: amma](https://ngrow.ai/cases/amma)
- Headspace caveat: the *perception* of fit doubled course starts, while the actual matching logic underperformed a default — [Irrational Labs](https://irrationallabs.com/case-studies/headspace-doubled-course-starts/)
- Apple 5.1.1(v): apps "may not require users to enter personal information to function, except when directly relevant to the core functionality." 5.1.1(ii) requires consent for data collection, and paid functionality must not depend on granting access to that data. Birth data and life-area questions should therefore be optional or plainly tied to the reading — [App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)

### Inferences
- ShenTarot already has the raw material: the chat-style question screen captures the user's intent. Reuse it in these places:
  - **Paywall headline**: e.g. "Keep exploring your question about [love/career]", feeding a life-area attribute into the paywall copy.
  - **Reading**: open the reading by restating the question and the chosen focus area.
  - **Day-1 and Day-3 push**: "Your Three of Cups reading on [topic], what's shifted?", a follow-up card draw.
  - **Home screen**: a "your ongoing question" thread.
- Pass birth date or zodiac sign (if added) into the AI prompt so readings feel more individual. Even light personalisation drives perceived fit, per Headspace.
- Keep push to a small number of relevant messages per week, since over-messaging raises churn.

### Gaps
- No public data on how much personalised readings or pushes lift *paid conversion* (as opposed to clicks) in astrology or tarot apps.
- The Airship and Braze push retention figures circulating online (e.g. 3.4x open rate, 34% higher 90-day retention) could not be verified at the original source and are omitted.

## Q4. What is the cheapest high-impact change for ShenTarot?

### Takeaway
Evidence-based recommendation, not a published result: add **one or two tap-to-answer intent questions** (life area plus a feeling/situation, optional birth date) to the existing short onboarding. Show a brief "tailoring your reading" reflection screen. Then **reuse those answers in the first free premium reading and in a personalised paywall shown right after that reading**, in the first session. This mostly reuses screens ShenTarot already has, and it maps onto the biggest effects in the evidence: perceived fit (about 2x core action in Headspace), first-session paywall exposure (about 3.7x per view vs later paywalls, about 90% of purchases on Day 0) and personalised paywall copy (used by only 2% of apps; 13–35% ARPU in vendor tests).

### Cited Findings
- Onboarding paywalls convert about 3.7x better per view than later paywalls, and 93% of first purchases happen within 24h — [Adapty State of Paywalls 2026](https://adapty.io/state-of-paywalls/report/)
- 82% of trials start on Day 0 — [RevenueCat SOSA 2025](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2025/)
- A personalisation quiz doubled the first core action even with identical content — [Irrational Labs / Headspace](https://irrationallabs.com/case-studies/headspace-doubled-course-starts/)
- 2% of paywall headlines use known user data. Personalised onboarding gave +13–35% ARPU in Adapty tests — [Adapty State of Paywalls 2026](https://adapty.io/state-of-paywalls/report/)
- PhotoRoom pattern: paywall immediately after the in-onboarding aha moment — [RevenueCat: paywall placement](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/paywall-placement/)
- Short-quiz guidance for funnels is 6–12 questions in 30–60 seconds, with results before pricing (lower-authority source) — [web-to-app funnel skill/guide](https://tessl.io/registry/skills/github/Eronred/aso-skills/web-to-app-funnel)
- 3.1.2(c) requires clearly describing what the user gets for the price before asking them to subscribe — [App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)

### Inferences
- Concrete minimal change set, in rough cost order:
  1. Personalised paywall headline and subtitle using the user's question or life area (copy change plus one attribute).
  2. Ensure the paywall is shown in the first session, immediately after the free premium spread result, and is dismissible to the 10 welcome credits.
  3. Add one or two intent questions (tap chips) before the first draw, then a 2–3 second reflection screen: "Focusing your reading on [career] and [uncertainty]...".
  4. Reference those answers in the AI reading prompt and in a single Day-1 follow-up push (with notification permission asked after the first reading, not upfront).
- Measure trial or intro-offer start rate per install on Day 0, first-reading completion, Day-1 return, and refund rate, so that conversion gains are not bought with refunds (hard paywalls carry about 70% more refunds per RevenueCat).
- The annual plan with a $1.99 first month resembles intro tactics criticised in Nebula reviews. Show the renewal price ($39.99/yr after the first month) beside the CTA.

### Gaps
- No tarot-specific A/B data exists publicly. The size of the effect for ShenTarot must be tested; the cross-category figures above are directional.
- Whether scan-a-physical-card (vs digital draw) changes time-to-aha or conversion is unstudied publicly.
