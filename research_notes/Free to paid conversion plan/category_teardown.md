# Competitor Teardown: How Tarot, Astrology and Spiritual-Wellness Apps Monetize Free Users

Research date: 2026-10-26. Sources are App Store US listings fetched on that date, unless noted. Adapty Paywall Library pages showed "latest info update" dates of 23-24 Oct 2026. Third-party revenue and download figures are **estimates**, and several pages contradict themselves (noted where it happens). App Store listings show IAP names and prices but usually **not** billing periods, trial length or credit-to-reading rates. Where a fact is missing it is listed as a gap, not inferred.

ShenTarot reference point: 10 welcome credits plus 1 free credit a day; credit packs US$1.99-5.99; Premium US$6.99/month, US$14.99/3 months, US$39.99/year, with a US$1.99 first month.

## Q1. Free tier, paywall triggers, prices, trials and credits for each app

### Takeaway
The category has three revenue models, and the big apps mix them:
- **Subscription plus à-la-carte consumables:** Co-Star, Labyrinthos, Nebula, The Pattern, Moonly.
- **Human-advisor marketplaces, paid per minute or per session:** Sanctuary, Kasamba, the chat add-ons in Nebula and Hint.
- **Weekly or monthly AI-tarot subscriptions:** clustered around US$6-10/week-or-month and US$50-60/year.

ShenTarot's credits plus Premium hybrid most closely matches Labyrinthos, the closest tarot comparable: credits from US$0.99, Premium US$9.99/month, US$24.99/quarter, US$89.99 (likely annual).

### Inferences
- **ShenTarot's pricing.** Premium at $6.99/month is below the main comparables: Labyrinthos $9.99, Co-Star $8.99, TarotAI $9.99, Arcara $9.99, Pattern $14.99. The $39.99 annual price is below Arcara ($59.99), Moonly ($59.99) and Hint ($49.99). ShenTarot is not underpriced against "cheap" AI tarot. It is at the low end, which leaves room to raise prices later or to stress value.
- **Credit packs.** The $1.99-5.99 packs sit inside the band set by Labyrinthos ($0.99-6.99) and Co-Star's question packs ($2.99-6.99). Labyrinthos's $19.99 "Starter Pack" suggests a higher-anchor bundle can sell in this category.
- **Weekly plans.** Weekly subscriptions ($4.99-9.99) are common among AI and astrology apps (Hint, Moonly, Tarot Cards Reading, Oraculous, reportedly Nebula). They are also the main source of "trial trap" complaints (see Q2). ShenTarot's choice to avoid weekly plans is defensible.
- **One-time options.** Lifetime or one-time unlocks show up in several apps: Moonly Lifetime $39.99, Tarotale $24.99, Stellium, and Labyrinthos "permanent upgrades." Some users clearly want a non-subscription path.
- **Paywall triggers in this category:**
  - a second person or relationship (Co-Star friend add, Pattern two-chart analysis, Nebula Relationship Check-In);
  - follow-up questions (Co-Star question packs, Arcara unlimited follow-ups);
  - deeper spreads (TarotAI);
  - saved history and journaling (TarotAI, Labyrinthos Reflections and notes);
  - live human chat (Nebula, Sanctuary, Hint, Kasamba).

### Gaps
- The trial length and introductory pricing for most subscriptions could not be confirmed from App Store listings. Listings show IAP names only, not the trial or the timing of the onboarding paywall.
- The credit-per-reading conversion is unknown for Labyrinthos (current), Nebula's chat balance, and Tarot Cards Reading's tokens.
- No pricing was found for Golden Thread Tarot (it may be fully free or the listing may be stale) or for Tarot by Trusted Tarot.
- No App Store listing was fetched for Lumi or Tarotoo specifically, so they are not covered.
- Billing periods for Nebula's $29.99, $39.99 and $49.99 tiers are unlabelled.

## Q2. What App Store reviews say about paying

### Takeaway
Negative reviews about paying focus on four things:
1. Features that used to be free are now locked ("now just another paywall").
2. Paying monthly for "AI" output feels overpriced or generic.
3. Trial auto-conversion, unclear pricing and hard cancellation.
4. Human-chat billing: timers, credits instead of refunds, no-shows.

Positive comments praise apps that are free and ad-free with fair one-time prices (Labyrinthos), detail that feels worth more than a human astrologer would charge (Nebula), and live human readings that felt engaging (Sanctuary).

- Sanctuary (2019): a subscription trial was "$20 well spent" and the live readings were "very engaging & informative." The reviewer did not renew because transcripts were not saved. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sanctuary-psychic-reading/id1417411962)
- Stellium: reviewers reportedly praise its one-time unlock over a subscription. — [Unstar blog, May 2026](https://unstar.app/blog/co-star-sanctuary-pattern-nebula-stellium-astrology-apps-ranked-2026)

### Inferences
- **Don't take away free value.** Removing something users had for free (Co-Star's daily horoscope and widget) causes the most resentment. ShenTarot's 1 free credit a day is a retention anchor; the inference is to keep it stable and never cut it later.
- **Price against the human alternative.** "AI" next to a monthly price makes users question value. The apps that do well frame it against a human reader ("cheaper than a psychic," "a personal astrologer would cost a fortune"), and they show specificity, avoiding generic card meanings.
- **Make the intro offer read cleanly.** Reviews punish trial-to-weekly auto-conversion and hard cancellation. A US$1.99 first month that renews to $6.99 needs clear disclosure of the renewal price and date, plus a reminder before renewal.
- **Save history.** Saved history and transcripts are a retention lever. A Sanctuary user cited their absence as the reason not to renew, and TarotAI gates saved history behind Pro.
- **Make credits safe.** Credit-loss-on-error complaints mean consumable credits need automatic refund on failed generations. ShenTarot's scan-plus-AI pipeline has its own failure points (bad scan, timeout).

### Gaps
- Most review evidence comes from the short review sample shown on App Store pages (3-4 reviews each) plus one third-party 2026 summary. No systematic sentiment counts were obtained, and justuseapp/AppFollow were not reached.
- No Reddit threads on paying for AI tarot apps came up in search.
- No reviews of Tarot by Trusted Tarot, Lumi or Tarotoo were retrieved.

## Q3. Which value moments convert in this category

### Takeaway
Based on what competitors gate and what reviewers pay for, these are the moments that monetize:
1. **Relationship or compatibility with a specific other person.** This is the most consistently gated: Co-Star, The Pattern, Nebula, Moonly.
2. **Asking a personal question or follow-up.** Co-Star sells question packs; Arcara sells unlimited follow-ups.
3. **Live human chat.** This is the highest price per minute: Kasamba, Sanctuary, Nebula, Hint.
4. **Long-horizon reports.** Year Ahead $11.99, Birth Chart $14.99, Love Connection $22.99.
5. **Depth tools:** advanced spreads, saved history, journaling and notes.

Daily horoscopes and daily cards are mainly the free hook and retention habit, not what users pay for.

### Cited Findings
- Co-Star monetizes questions, the charts of other people, love reports, couples (Eros) and Year Ahead. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/co-star-personalized-astrology/id1264782561)
- The Pattern gates relationship analysis and sells "Connect" products and profile slots, which are products for adding other people. — [Adapty](https://adapty.io/paywall-library/the-pattern/); [Unstar](https://unstar.app/blog/co-star-sanctuary-pattern-nebula-stellium-astrology-apps-ranked-2026)
- Nebula sells a Relationship Check-In ($4.99), "Daily secrets from birth chart" ($7.99), chat balance ($9.99) and a Love Connection Reading ($22.99). — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/nebula-spiritual-guidance/id1459969523); [App Store (other storefront)](https://apps.apple.com/lr/app/nebula-horoscope-astrology/id1459969523)
- Labyrinthos gates unlimited personalized (AI) readings, Reflections, notes and Clarifying Cards. Learning content and decks stay free. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1155180220)
- TarotAI gates extra spreads, the significator, "enhanced" AI and saved history. Daily guidance stays free. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tarotai-daily-card-readings/id6447095993)

- Co-Star uses daily-horoscope notifications as upsell surfaces (reported). — [Unstar](https://unstar.app/blog/co-star-sanctuary-pattern-nebula-stellium-astrology-apps-ranked-2026)

### Inferences
- **Paywall moments for ShenTarot:**
  - a follow-up question after a free reading;
  - a relationship or "about someone else" spread;
  - longer spreads such as the Celtic Cross;
  - a "year or month ahead" reading;
  - the reading journal and history.
- **What to keep free:** the daily single card and scan novelty, as the habit hook.
- **Physical-card scanning.** No competitor seen scans physical cards. Labyrinthos and Golden Thread let users log physical-card readings manually. This sets ShenTarot apart in the "own deck" segment that Labyrinthos and Golden Thread serve.

### Gaps
- No public conversion-rate data by feature was found for any app in the category. The ranking above is inferred from gating patterns and price levels, not from measured conversion.

## Q4. Revenue and download estimates (all are ESTIMATES)

### Takeaway
Public figures are scarce and contradictory. ### Gaps

- No revenue estimates were found for Labyrinthos, Sanctuary, Hint, Kasamba or any AI tarot app.
