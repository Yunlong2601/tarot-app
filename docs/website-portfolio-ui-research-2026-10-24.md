# ShenTarot: usable interaction ideas from established design leaders

Research date: 24 October 2026. This document follows the owner's request to study famous art directors, UI/UX designers and award-winning portfolio sites, extracting elements that can serve ShenTarot.

Source refresh: 25 October 2026 (Singapore). Reopened all ten homepages through web research and checked selected award/organizer records. The screenshots and interactive observations below remain the 24 October browser inspection; this refresh is not a new interaction audit. Active Theory's text fetch exposes only a JavaScript requirement, and Anton & Irene's exposes its title; use the earlier captured browser observations for their visual details. The One Club Lusion archive returned 403 during the refresh; its historical standing is also documented by [CSS Design Awards' 2023 winners](https://www.cssdesignawards.com/blog/2023-website-of-the-year-winners/394/) and [Lusion's own award record](https://lusion.co/about/).

The recommendation is a paper-and-ink reading product with expressive card presentation, a clear visual index and calm writing surfaces. The portfolio references enrich the approved direction. They do not replace the existing browser journey or establish new AI, photo upload, sync or commercial capabilities.

There is no fixed exhaustive list of “artistic UI elements.” The catalogue below covers 64 useful patterns, treatments and optional experiments. Many combine with each other. **An adaptation attributed to a reference is our proposed translation of a principle; it is not a claim that the reference already has a tarot picker, writing tool or AI composer.**

## Observed details and inspection limits

- **Pentagram:** opened the work-type filter. Its two choices are embedded in “We design [Everything] for [Everyone].” The expanded filter presents named discipline choices. This is a practical model for making a catalogue feel editorial without abandoning explicit selections.
- **&Walsh:** viewed large image tiles with project titles and opened its navigation. Work, Info and Contact sit beside a prominent sculptural ampersand. The useful principle is a small recognizable visual motif surrounding a short destination list. Its observed control semantics are not a production accessibility template.
- **COLLINS:** the current homepage uses a centered serif statement on a light surface, followed by Programs and Case Studies. The [San Francisco Symphony case](https://wearecollins.com/case-studies/san-francisco-symphony/) explains responsive variable typography. That is a documented project system, not a claim that the homepage's body text reacts to sound.
- **Studio Dumbar:** opened its OpenAI case. The [published explanation](https://studiodumbar.com/work/openai) describes restrained, poetic motion and applied creative coding. Multiple video embeds reported “Unable to play media” in this session; detailed timing and those videos were not visually verified.
- **Sagmeister:** viewed the artwork-led catalogue and inspected the published [Beautiful Numbers explanation](https://sagmeister.com/work/beautiful-numbers/) and [Answers](https://sagmeister.com/answers/) categories. An unsolicited newsletter overlay appeared; ShenTarot should not adopt that interruption during reading.
- **Lusion:** observed the light lavender shell around a dark black/white/cobalt 3D stage, then opened the layered navigation. It separates ordinary destinations from contact and Labs. The final captured menu uses opaque white and black panels on a saturated field; translucent overlap was visible during its entrance. Transfer the bounded stage, grouping and readable final surfaces.
- **Locomotive:** observed the current intro and opened the narrow-layout navigation: large serif Work, Agency, Careers, Let's talk and Store on a saturated blue field, with a language link below. The useful lesson is hierarchy and target size, rather than the exact color or typography.
- **Anton & Irene:** opened Work. A tall dark project index appears over the hero, with project names and discipline subtitles. This is a particularly useful discovery pattern for preserving context behind an index.
- **Active Theory:** its loaded scene showed metallic looping forms, particles and a compact Work/Contact utility pill. Activating Work produced a page-script error in this session; navigation success was not established. Use it for atmosphere, not as verified navigation behavior.
- **Bruno Simon:** observed the current 3D start scene and “Click to start” cue. The [current page text](https://bruno-simon.com/) documents quality, audio, reset, recovery and multiple input controls. Those controls were not all operated. Full gaming navigation is unsuitable for a visitor who simply needs a card meaning.

Browser inspection used the available desktop/narrow browser surface. No physical-phone, screen-reader, full keyboard, performance or user-study audit of these references was completed. Some sites show entry/load transitions before content; this session did not measure their load times. Awards and visual appeal do not establish usability, conversion improvement or accessibility conformance.

## How to combine the references coherently

Use Pentagram and Anton & Irene to organize information, COLLINS to set editorial pace, &Walsh to establish a memorable original motif, and Lusion/Studio Dumbar to animate the card story. Use Bruno Simon's recovery principle to keep the experience understandable when effects are disabled.

Retain the approved parchment #F4F0E7, ink #17171B, forest #243C35, muted gold #B69A60 and aubergine #302332. Keep the existing editorial serif and control/body sans rather than importing a different typeface from every reference. Gold remains decorative until checked for its particular use.

Use a small motion vocabulary: press, open, place, reveal and connect. Reserve the longer movement for a card reveal; everyday controls respond quickly. Avoid combining a custom cursor, magnetic buttons, continuous particles, kinetic headings and elaborate scroll choreography in the same task area.

## Functional guidance

Portfolio styling does not replace functional specifications. Use ordinary navigation links and disclosure semantics as described by [W3C disclosure navigation](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/disclosure/examples/disclosure-navigation/), with [combobox](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/combobox/) and [modal dialog](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/dialog-modal/) guidance for enhanced discovery controls. APG examples themselves require real assistive-technology testing before production use.

Keep click/tap alternatives for reordering; keyboard support alone does not satisfy the [WCAG 2.2 AA dragging criterion](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/dragging-movements.html). Prefer 44–48px main action targets as a design choice; the [AA target-size criterion](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html) specifies 24px with exceptions.

Honor reduced motion and retain a visible control. [Animation from Interactions](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/animation-from-interactions.html) is a Level AAA criterion; do not mislabel it as an AA-only requirement. Native scrolling, immediately readable content and clear focus remain essential.

Supporting references, rather than the main portfolio shortlist: [Linear search](https://linear.app/docs/search), [Tally input blocks](https://tally.so/help/input-blocks), [GOV.UK character count](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/character-count/), [GOV.UK error message](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/error-message/), [Emil Kowalski's practical animation tips](https://emilkowal.ski/ui/7-practical-animation-tips), [Rauno Freiberg's interaction-design essay](https://rauno.me/craft/interaction-design), and [React Bits Circular Gallery](https://reactbits.dev/components/circular-gallery). Their inclusion supports specific behaviors or experiments; it is not a claim that all are equally famous.

## Research status

The catalogue and recommendations are complete for this research pass. No consumer preference, conversion gain, physical-device result or measured production-performance claim follows from it. No product source, deployment, store settings, production AI or tracking changed in this research phase. The broader implementation goal remains open; its outstanding validation is recorded in the interaction verification document.
