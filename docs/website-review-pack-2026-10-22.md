## Disputed choices to assess

1. Reversals can be blocked/inward/excessive/delayed rather than automatically bad or opposite. Are the examples clear without asserting one compulsory tradition?
2. The same Eight of Pentacles can suggest practice as advice or perfectionism as a challenge. Is this a defensible possibility, rather than a claim about every querent?
3. Does the Magician → Two reversed → Eight connection follow visible details and positions without moralising about discipline?
4. Do elemental/astrological correspondences state their tradition and acknowledge variation? Avoid presenting symbolism as factual astronomy.
5. Could readers confuse editorial demonstrations with personalised AI output? Check labels and calls to action.
6. Are local saving, overwrite behaviour and lack of account sync understandable?

## Correction record

For each suggestion record: page URL, language, card/section, short exact passage, issue category (factual/source, tradition-dependent, unclear, translation, accessibility), rationale/source, proposed replacement, confidence, reviewer identity and permission to attribute. Keep personal readings, birth data and photos out of the record.

Existing contact route: the Support email linked from methodology. The new correction section explains these steps and opens a mail composer; no message is sent automatically. A submitted suggestion is not an acceptance or endorsement. The owner triages the item, verifies sources, changes the shared source only through the appropriate code owner when necessary, runs translations/tests and reports the result. No fake public review badge is added.

A native speaker should review the translated additions after the English concepts are accepted. Review source text and rendered 320px pages, not only JSON keys. Publish reviewer names or quotes only after both actual review and permission.
