# ShenTarot consumer and competitor research

Reviewed 21 October 2026. Recommendations below are hypotheses to validate with ShenTarot visitors, not demonstrated conversion improvements. No website or backend changes were made in this research phase.

## Product decision

Serve the reader who has cards on the table and wants to understand them. The proposed journey is: find a meaning → apply it to a spread → explain connections → save a reflection → return for practice. Preserve existing artwork and content. Build on the current card, context, spread, learning and zodiac pages rather than rebuilding their libraries.

## Competitors and observable methods

- **Labyrinthos: closest product benchmark.** Its Google Play listing shows 1M+ downloads and describes physical-card entry, short quizzes, journaling, reminders and optional paid AI. Its Star meaning page combines quick keyword tables, longer contextual explanations and an app invitation. These show an acquisition-to-practice path; they do not establish its conversion rate or profitability. Adopt layered explanations and a clear next action from each meaning page.
  - https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.labyrinthos.app&hl=en_US
  - https://labyrinthos.co/blogs/tarot-card-meanings-list/the-star-meaning-major-arcana-tarot-card-meanings
- **Biddy Tarot: education benchmark.** Its About page self-reports 15 million annual visitors and 16,000 program students; these are unaudited marketing figures. Its resources connect free guides and training to a weekly newsletter and paid education. Adopt a short beginner pathway and useful take-home reference; defer a newsletter until ongoing publishing and delivery are supported.
  - https://biddytarot.com/about/
  - https://biddytarot.com/free-tarot-resources/
- **Tarot.com: return-visit benchmark.** Membership combines saved readings, daily content, newsletters, a personal profile and credits. Adopt continuity around a saved reading and a useful weekly practice. Do not assume its commercial results are public.
  - https://www.tarot.com/member/benefits

These are relevance-based benchmarks, not a verified traffic ranking of every tarot competitor. Study their mechanisms; create original explanations, diagrams and designs.

## Research and limits

- **Pew Research Center, 2025 report:** survey of 9,593 US adults, fielded October 2024. Thirty percent consulted astrology, tarot or fortune tellers at least annually; enjoyment was more common than seeking guidance. This supports offering a welcoming, exploratory experience. It does not estimate Singapore demand, willingness to pay or ShenTarot retention.
  - https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/2025/05/21/3-in-10-americans-consult-astrology-tarot-cards-or-fortune-tellers/
- **Interpretive Cultures, 2026 manuscript:** interviews with 12 North American tarot practitioners already using AI. Participants sought clarification and alternative perspectives but raised concerns about agreement-seeking AI and losing interpretive agency. Offer an optional first impression, visible reasoning and alternative readings. This small selected sample cannot represent all hobbyists or prove an effect on retention.
  - https://arxiv.org/html/2602.11367v1
- **The Power of Feedback Revisited, 2020:** educational feedback meta-analysis spanning 435 studies. Results depend on feedback characteristics; application to tarot is indirect. Test explanations of symbolism and position, rather than generic praise. Do not grade personal interpretations as objectively right or wrong.
  - https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2019.03087/full

## Prioritised acceptance checklist

- [ ] Card search accepts names and useful variants; empty results offer recovery. Meanings remain available without an account.
- [ ] A visitor can select physical cards, orientations and spread positions without drawing replacement cards. Preserve the selection throughout the flow.
- [ ] Each priority spread has a legible numbered diagram, an example question and a worked interpretation.
- [ ] AI explains the card, its position and at least one connection; identifies interpretations as possibilities; preserves the user's question and original cards in follow-ups. Show cost before submission. AI integration requires an assigned backend owner.
- [ ] The user can add an optional first impression or skip it. Reference meanings remain usable without AI.
- [ ] Saving states clearly whether notes stay on this browser or sync to an account. Test persistence and provide export before presenting it as a dependable journal.
- [ ] Extend existing zodiac pages with reviewed weekly tarot reflections. Label symbolic astrology separately from factual sky events. Date-dependent astronomy must use verified calculations, not generated dates.
- [ ] Verify keyboard use and narrow mobile layouts for the complete journey before deployment. Human comprehension needs participant testing in addition to automated checks.

## Consumer validation before expanding

Recruit 5–8 consenting hobbyists across beginner and intermediate experience, including people who dislike AI. Ask them to find a reversed card, choose a suitable spread, enter their own cards, explain one connection and save a note. Observe task completion, hesitation, recovery and understanding. Do not contact participants automatically.

Initial usability targets, not research-derived guarantees: at least 4 of the first 5 participants complete each core task unaided; card lookup takes under 30 seconds; participants can explain a connection in their own words. Fix observed blockers before increasing page volume.

For a later analytics experiment, establish a baseline for meaning-page-to-tool starts, completed interpretations, useful-result feedback and seven-day return visits. Define denominators and compare comparable traffic sources. Use aggregate events without recording questions or journal text; account for the current site's privacy promises before enabling measurement. At low volume, use interviews rather than treating noisy conversion changes as proof.
