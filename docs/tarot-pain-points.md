# 10 pain points in the tarot community, and what ShenTarot does about them

Researched 2026-10-23. Most of the evidence is app reviews, forum posts and tarot teachers' blogs. That makes it anecdotal, not survey data, but the same complaints keep coming up.

Legend: ✅ ShenTarot already solves it · 🟡 partly · 🔨 worth building

## People getting readings (users)

### 1. Readings feel generic and copy-pasted
- **The pain.** Many apps show the same stock card definition whatever you asked. One EvaTarot reviewer said the scripts kept repeating, even across different decks ([Trustpilot](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/evatarot.net)). Competitors now market themselves as the cure for "generic definitions" ([Arcana Oracle](https://www.producthunt.com/products/arcana-oracle)).
- **ShenTarot.** ✅ The AI reads the user's own question, each card's position and how the cards relate, not just one card's meaning.
- **Next.** 🔨 Under each card, add one line saying *why* it matters for this question. That makes the "not generic" claim visible.

### 2. Bait paywalls and trial tricks
- **The pain.** In one app, free users could only open The Fool. Another reviewer was charged at once for a "3-day free trial" ([Lumi reviews](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/6450309122/tarot-cards-reading-meanings/reviews)). Users also dislike flashing subscription nags ([Labyrinthos reviews](https://splitmetrics.com/apps/labyrinthos-tarot-reading/id1155180220)).
- **ShenTarot.** ✅ All 78 card meanings are free. Everyone gets free daily credits. The paywall shows the price, the billing period and the renewal terms. Credit packs (1.0.1) let people pay without subscribing.
- **Next.** Say this in the App Store listing: "All 78 cards free. No trial tricks."

### 3. "I own a real deck, but apps make me tap virtual cards"
- **The pain.** Most apps draw digital cards. People who shuffle a physical deck have to search for each card by hand, which kills the ritual.
- **ShenTarot.** ✅ This is our core difference. You scan one card on the phone, or photograph the whole spread and the AI names every card and whether it's reversed.
- **Next.** 🔨 Make this the first screenshot and the first line of the store listing.

### 4. Anxious re-asking of the same question
- **The pain.** Teachers describe clients who ask the same question again and again, often from distress. They suggest a gap between readings on the same question ([The Tarot Lady](https://www.thetarotlady.com/asking-tarot-the-same-question-again-and-again/)).
- **ShenTarot.** 🟡 Daily credits limit this indirectly.
- **Next.** 🔨 When a question looks like one asked recently, show a gentle note: "You asked this 2 days ago. Here's what came up." This is caring and builds trust, and it also saves AI cost.

### 5. Distrust of AI: "it just tells me what I want to hear"
- **The pain.** Practitioners worry AI is sycophantic and weakens people's own judgement ([Fortune, 12 readers](https://fortune.com/2026/05/16/we-talked-to-12-tarot-card-readers-who-are-using-ai-they-split-in-2-camps-with-big-implications-for-the-technology/), [PBS](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/amp/arts/more-tarot-readers-are-turning-to-ai-for-advice-another-sign-of-our-growing-reliance-on-chatbots-for-emotional-support)).
- **ShenTarot.** 🟡 The reading prompt asks for a balanced reading and for hard cards to be named honestly.
- **Next.** 🔨 Give every reading a short "the challenge here" section, so it never reads as pure flattery.

### 6. Privacy: the questions are intimate
- **The pain.** People ask about love, health, money and family. Many apps require an account and store everything.
- **ShenTarot.** ✅ No account is needed, and history stays on the phone. Single-card scans never leave the device. A spread photo is sent once, then deleted.
- **Next.** Put this in the listing as well: "Your readings stay on your phone."

## People learning to read (readers)

### 7. Overwhelm: 78 cards, reversals and court cards
- **The pain.** Biddy Tarot surveyed more than 600 people. Court cards, reversals and combinations were their biggest struggles, along with feeling they had to know everything before they were "allowed" to read ([Biddy Tarot](https://biddytarot.com/blog/3-common-myths-of-learning-tarot/)). Beginners find memorizing overwhelming ([r/Tarotpractices](https://redlib.hbubli.cc/r/Tarotpractices/comments/1h8154d/questions_about_tarot)). Teachers recommend short keywords over memorizing whole pages ([The Tarot Lady](https://www.thetarotlady.com/is-it-a-good-idea-to-memorize-what-tarot-cards-mean/)).
- **ShenTarot.** ✅ The Learn tab has short keyword meanings in 16 languages, plus a guide to spreads.
- **Next.** 🔨 Add a 30-second daily "card of the day" quiz to build streaks and habit.

### 8. "AI defeats the point of learning"
- **The pain.** One Labyrinthos reviewer said an AI interpretation defeats the purpose of learning to read the cards yourself ([reviews](https://splitmetrics.com/apps/labyrinthos-tarot-reading/id1155180220)).
- **ShenTarot.** 🔨 Not yet.
- **Next.** 🔨 Add a "Read it myself first" toggle. The user types their own interpretation, then the AI compares it with theirs. That turns the AI into a teacher, which wins over the learners who reject AI tarot.

### 9. No memory of past readings or patterns
- **The pain.** Readers pay for tools that track recurring cards and themes. Mystic Mondays sells "Insights" by subscription, and TarotLingo and Arcanas sell pattern charts ([Mystic Mondays](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mystic-mondays/id1233064572), [TarotLingo](https://mwm.ai/apps/id/6746827482), [Arcanas](https://apps.apple.com/kr/app/arcanas-ai-%ED%83%80%EB%A1%9C-%EB%A6%AC%EB%94%A9/id6737407513?l=en-GB)). Others buy Notion templates just for this ([Etherea](https://etherea.gumroad.com/l/tarot_journal/INTUITIVEYOU)).
- **ShenTarot.** 🟡 Every reading is saved in History, and Your journey shows streaks.
- **Next.** 🔨 Add a premium "Your cards this month" recap: the cards that keep appearing and what the theme is. This is the strongest reason to keep subscribing, and it costs one cheap AI call a month.

### 10. Professional readers: burnout, pricing and client notes
- **The pain.** Readers burn out after a few readings a day, underprice their work, and keep notes on clients by hand ([Benebell Wen](https://benebellwen.com/2013/11/14/), [Biddy Tarot](https://biddytarot.com/blog/5-signs-its-time-to-raise-your-tarot-reading-prices/), [Tarot Journal app](https://mwm.ai/apps/id/1271120458)).
- **ShenTarot.** Not our market for 1.0.
- **Next.** Later, a "Reader mode": scan a client's spread, get a draft summary and save it under the client's name. A higher price tier, after the consumer app proves itself.
