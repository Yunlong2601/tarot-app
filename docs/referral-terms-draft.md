# Refer-and-earn terms (DRAFT for legal review; not published, not in the app's Terms yet)

Status 2026-10-17: draft. Add to the Terms of Use only when referrals are switched on. Some countries regulate promotions; have this reviewed before launch.

**Invite friends programme**
1. **Who can take part.** Anyone using ShenTarot, subject to these Terms. Friends must be new to ShenTarot Premium (the store decides eligibility for its offers).
2. **Friend's offer.** A friend who enters your code gets 50% off the first year of the annual plan when they subscribe (for example US$19.99, then the full annual price each year after). Nothing is given for installing the app or entering a code. The offer is applied by the App Store (one-time offer code) or Google Play (in-app offer); their terms and the price shown before purchase apply. One friend's offer per person.
2a. **Credits.** When a friend enters your code and then finishes their first reading, you and your friend each receive 10 reading credits. You are rewarded for at most 20 friends in any 12 months; a friend always receives theirs. Credits are not paid for installing the app or entering a code. Credits have no cash value and are explained in the Terms of Use.
3. **Your reward.** For each friend who pays for a subscription, you get 1 free month of Premium, added 14 days after their first payment if it has not been refunded. At most 6 free months in any 12 months. If you already subscribe in the store, earned months are kept and added after your subscription ends. Free months have no cash value and cannot be transferred.
4. **Tiers.** Tiers (Seeker, Adept, Mystic, Oracle) unlock cosmetic extras such as card backs, icons and themes. Cosmetics may change; they have no cash value.
5. **Fair play.** No self-referrals, fake accounts, paid promotion of codes in app-store reviews, or spam. We may cancel codes, offers or rewards obtained against these rules, and end or change the programme at any time; rewards already added stay.
6. **Without an account.** Codes and rewards belong to the phone they were created on and are lost if the app is deleted. Sign in to keep them across phones.
7. **Data.** See the Privacy Policy: we keep your code, which friends used it (by random ID, never names or contacts), the store transaction that qualified a referral, and the dates rewards were added.

## Privacy policy addition (draft, add when switched on)
- **Invite friends (optional).** If you use invites, we keep a random invite code for your account or phone, a link between your random ID and the random IDs of friends who used your code, the store transaction ID of a friend's qualifying purchase (to confirm a real payment and prevent duplicates), and the dates free months were added.
