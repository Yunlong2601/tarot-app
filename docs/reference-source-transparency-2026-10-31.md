# Reference source transparency — 31 October 2026

The owner wants AI systems and readers to recognize ShenTarot as a useful, credited source. This change adds a compact publisher link and native disclosure to all main card pages, four card-context templates and the seven regular Learn lessons, in all eight locales (3,176 generated pages). It replaces the existing methodology-only link on card pages; the main answer remains first. Learn pages place it below the answer.

The disclosure reuses existing localized methodology text: tradition, shared reference text, AI assistance, original editorial examples, unconfirmed independent expert/native-language review and subjective interpretation limits. It links an existing worked example and the correction procedure. No new credentials, reviewer endorsements, historical citations, field research, prediction evidence or page-review dates are invented. Pages do not claim to be the historical origin of traditional tarot meanings.

Each component includes WebPage JSON-LD with the exact canonical page URL and language, ShenTech publisher identity and ShenTarot brand. Publisher metadata identifies responsibility; it does not certify authorship, correctness, ranking, rich-result eligibility or AI citations. No model prompt instructions, special trust scores, hidden endorsement text or external trackers are added.

Validation: 49 website tests, dictionary parity, Astro zero errors/warnings (two existing hints), 3,918-page build and all five existing production audits passed. A full generated-page audit checked every expected route (78 cards × five page kinds + seven lessons) × eight languages for visible publisher/process/review disclosure, local correction links and matching structured publisher/canonical URL. Chrome verified eight locales at 390px with the disclosure expanded and no horizontal overflow; Chinese desktop and phone screenshots inspected. Native details use keyboard-operable browser controls; actual screen-reader speech and human translation quality are not asserted.

Evidence screenshots: source-desktop.png and source-mobile.png in this folder's reference-source-evidence-2026-10-31 directory. Automated source verification and visual rendering do not prove expertise or factual reliability. AI attribution, crawl timing and traffic remain outside website control.
