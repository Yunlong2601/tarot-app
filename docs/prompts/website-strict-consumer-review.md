# Strict consumer viewpoint: ShenTarot

Purpose: an evidence-based agent usability assessment, not interviews, analytics, testimonials or proof that consumers agree. The owner confirmed that Search Console results, reviewer feedback and consenting testers are not available for this run. Keep the real 5–8-person comparison study pending; never replace it with fictional participants.

## Prompt

Evaluate ShenTarot as a beginner/intermediate hobbyist who has three physical tarot cards on the table. My goal is to understand this draw, not browse a large feature catalogue. I have no obligation to sign up, install an upcoming app, pay, or return. Judge what I can actually complete today.

Use this exact fictional task: “How can I make room for a creative project?” Situation: Magician upright; Challenge: Two of Pentacles reversed; Way forward: Eight of Pentacles upright. Compare public/already-authorised Labyrinthos, Biddy Tarot and FlipTarot routes for the relevant task. Stop at gates. Do not substitute random cards, assume advertised performance or copy content.

For each of the nine questions below, record visible evidence/URL, the consumer's unmet job, observed effort/barrier, implemented or proposed fix, test criterion and any dependency. Mark pass/partial/fail/untested only against a stated observable criterion. Do not invent satisfaction scores, timings, preferences, traffic numbers or conversion uplift. Thoughts in first person are hypothetical review lenses, never customer quotes.

1. DISCOVERY — “Would this answer the exact query I searched?” Check indexing/performance only through authorised reports. Without them, visibility stays untested. On the page, check whether the requested card/reversal has a direct, usable answer. Don't equate more pages with demand.
2. REACH — “How would I hear about this beyond its creator?” Identify an actual distribution experiment and whether it ran. Prepared assets are not acquisition. No posting, contacting or ad buying without separate authorisation.
3. EXPLANATION — “Can this explain my question and my cards today?” Distinguish a generic reference, editorial example and actual AI response. Cost/access and limits must be clear before submission. If browser auth/preflight/charging safety is missing, report a blocker rather than a fake working CTA.
4. ENTRY — “Why must I type everything, and can I fix mistakes?” Enter the actual physical draw, reverse/correct/reorder without duplicates or note loss. Photo recognition is only an advantage if available and tested with appropriate permission; never invent scanning or upload personal photos.
5. TRUST — “Who wrote this and why should I believe it?” Check honest sources, methodology, qualification/review status, correction route and privacy. Transparency isn't independent endorsement. Never fabricate reviews or label tarot predictions scientifically validated.
6. UNDERSTANDING — “I know three definitions; how do they become one interpretation?” Require visible guidance for all confirmed cards and their correct positions/orientations, a connection, an alternative and a practical reflection. Prompts are not personalised AI. A first-two-cards-only panel fails this complete-spread criterion.
7. LEARNING — “What can I practise next, and what did I learn?” Follow the existing recommendation. Observe whether the person can explain a visual detail/position/alternative; a self-marked checkbox alone does not prove comprehension. Never grade subjective readings as objectively right.
8. RETURN — “Will my work still be here, and is returning worthwhile?” Leave for a lesson, resume the same draw, cancel an overwrite, refresh/reopen and recover what was actually saved. Make one-snapshot, tab-draft and cross-device limits explicit. Preserve Premium history/journal/look-back boundaries. No fake sync, reminders or newsletters.
9. CHOICE — “Why use this instead of my booklet, Google or another app?” Demonstrate a specific finished job and its remaining limits. Use the consenting counterbalanced study when actual participants become available. Do not assert preference or superiority from an agent walkthrough.

Test 320px/390px layouts, long translations, keyboard/focus, meaningful storage failures and copy/export/print/delete. Preserve all eight locales, current URLs, artwork and app/backend/pricing/entitlements. Record native/expert/device/OS-print limits honestly. No private reading content in analytics.

Implement evidence-supported independent fixes, not more decorative sections or repetitive SEO pages. Read current main, merged #23–#25 and research first. Preserve work in an isolated branch. For meaningful website changes, run checks, update PROGRESS/evidence, open a focused PR, merge after passing checks, deploy website only and verify production. Publication is authorised. Report live URL, merge SHA, deployment/version/rollback, evidence and external dependencies.

## Observed application in this release

- Complete-spread criterion initially failed: live connection text named Magician and Two only, omitting Eight. The fix guides every selected position in order, includes its actual orientation, asks for a whole-spread alternative/action, and carries prompts into export/copy/print preparation.
- Return criterion was partial: on the live worked example, the only workspace link seeded the example again. The existing validated session-resume component is reused on the example, pocket guide, practice page, two beginner lessons and four selected card references. It returns without seeding or overwriting the draw.
- Discovery/reach/choice remain unproven without reports, launched distribution or real participants. AI/scan/account capabilities are not created by this release. Credibility still awaits independent and native review.
